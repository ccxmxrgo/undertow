Notes
-----
- No OGG encoder (oggenc/ffmpeg) is present on the build machine and none was
  installed. The WAV is kept as the repository source and imported by Godot
  with compress/mode=2 (QOA); the imported sample is about 6.8 MB instead of
  33.9 MB, so the exported pck stays small. The MP3 is imported as-is.
- Charts (notes.chart) and song.ini files are original work, generated from the audio
  by tools/autochart (UNDERTOW autochart), see tools/autochart/README.txt.
