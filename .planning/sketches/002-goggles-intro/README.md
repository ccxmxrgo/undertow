# Sketch 002: Goggles Intro

## Design Question
How does the goggles-on transition (goggles slide in, CRT-style blink, world turns on, count-in) feel at full length (every dive) and quick (Retry)?

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/002-goggles-intro/index.html. Use ↻ Replay; any key skips.

## Variants
- **A: Cinematic (every dive).** Room, goggles slide on, black, CRT dot→line→picture with "LINK ESTABLISHED", the arena fades in and the board grows from the horizon, then 3-2-1, DIVE. About 4 s.
- **B: Quick (Retry).** CRT blink plus a single count. Under 1 s.

## What to Look For
- Does it make you feel like you're jacking in?
- Is the length right? Does the skip feel responsive?
- Room look and goggles design are placeholders for the mood, not final art.
