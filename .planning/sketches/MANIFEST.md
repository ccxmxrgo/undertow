# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
"Put on the goggles and you're a rockstar in another world" (Ready Player One), with the braindance FEEL: immersive and sensory, not cluttered UI. You dive into a neon abyss arena. Screen edges stay clean, and the style lives on the board itself as curved neon lines on a 3D road toward a glowing horizon. It must NOT feel like Guitar Hero. Lane neons are cyan ◆, amber ●, magenta ▲, lime ■.

## Reference Points
Ready Player One (putting on the goggles, entering the OASIS); Cyberpunk 2077 braindance (feel); deep-sea bioluminescence; old CRT TV switching on.
