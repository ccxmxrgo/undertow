# Sketch 001: Board Concepts

## Design Question
Which 3D board diving toward a glowing horizon feels like UNDERTOW, not Guitar Hero (no plank, no frets, no flames)? Screen edges stay clean, and the HUD floats near the board.

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/001-board-concepts/index.html. Click to start, press 1 2 3 4 to play (or leave Autoplay on).

## Variants
- **A: Sonar Arc.** No board surface. Four light tendrils curve into the abyss, the hit line is a curved sonar arc, and sonar ping rings travel down and land exactly on each beat.
- **B: Current Ribbon.** A translucent current that sways and banks with the music. Notes are glowing bioluminescent orbs and the edges are soft curved neon.
- **C: Trench Descent.** You look down a breathing trench. Notes rise out of the depth along cables to a ring near you.

## What to Look For
- Which one makes you feel "inside another world" rather than "in a rhythm game menu"?
- Readability: can you tell when to hit? (Notes glow only inside the hit window.)
- Which one feels least like Guitar Hero?
- Is the floating HUD (streak left, depth right, hull arc underneath) enough?
