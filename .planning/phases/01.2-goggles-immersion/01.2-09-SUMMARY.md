**2. [Rule 1 - Harness] The off-line bound includes the lane's x drift**
- The plan's formula TRAVEL * |n'(0)| / approach is vertical only, and outer lanes also move in x.
- The bound now uses a numeric derivative of `Trench.point` at d = 0 for the lane, in px per unit depth, scaled by (1 - P|nd|)^-2 for notes just past the line, plus 0.5 px.
- The vertical px/s value is recorded as `line_speed_y_px_per_s_1x`.

**3. Receptor level check method**

- A brightest-row scan just right of each receptor confirms the line row.
