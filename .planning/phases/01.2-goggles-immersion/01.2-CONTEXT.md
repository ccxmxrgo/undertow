<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Make a dive feel like putting on goggles and entering another world:
- a cinematic goggles-on intro
- the gameplay board rebuilt as the **Trench Descent** 3D view
- a reactive neon-abyss arena around it
- clean screen edges with no frame chrome

Then ship playtest v0.2, which also carries the Phase 1.1 polish.

**Not in this phase:**
- real songs/import (Phase 2) and instrument extraction (Phase 2.1)
- torpedoes/versus (Phase 3), online play (Phase 4)
- the sonar-ping/creak audio suite (Phase 5 AV-03)
- difficulty presets

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Core fantasy (locked, user)
- **D-01:** "Put on the goggles and you're a rockstar in another world" (Ready Player One). The goggles jack you INTO a virtual neon abyss arena. Torpedoes, hull and depth stay, as that world's rules. Braindance = the immersive FEEL, not cluttered UI.

### Board: Trench Descent (locked, sketch 001 winner C)
- **D-02:** Reference implementation: `.planning/sketches/001-board-concepts/index.html`, variant C (`P.c` projection + `boardC`). The look:
  - **Viewpoint:** looking down a trench. The vanishing point sits near the upper centre (sketch: 800,330 on 1600×900).
  - **Cables:** 4 lanes are cables descending from the glowing depth. They are dark cable cores with a dimmed lane-colour line, a slight sag, and signal pulses running along them on each beat.
  - **Notes:** they rise out of the depth along the cables toward a **curved hit ring near the bottom** (an upward-curved arc; sketch hit y≈800).
  - **Trench walls:** jagged rock rings scroll toward the player and **breathe on the beat** (scale pulse about 3.5%). Rings alternate teal and purple and brighten when near.
  - **Particles:** marine-snow particles drift.
- **D-03:** Notes keep the lane colours and shapes from 1.1: cyan ◆, amber ●, magenta ▲, lime ■. They stay dim until inside the Good window, then turn bright with a glow. Receptors are outline shapes on the hit ring, numbered 1–4 underneath, and pulse on press. Note size scales with perspective.
- **D-04:** Timing integrity (non-negotiable, Core Value). Depth along the cable is **linear in time**: `d = (note_time - song_time) / approach_time`, and only the perspective projection is non-linear. A note's centre crosses the hit ring exactly at its judge time (visual offset applied as in Phase 1). Scroll speed maps to approach time. Autoplay must stay all-Perfect, and the existing judgment/clock code must not change behaviour.
- **D-05:** Not Guitar Hero: no plank, no frets, no flames or star power. If the board ever looks like a fretboard, it's wrong.

### Clean edges + minimal HUD (locked, user)
- **D-06:** Remove the 01.1-06 frame chrome: the red rim lines, rulers, compass strip, layer chips, timeline scrubber bar, bottom status strip and corner brackets. Screen edges are clean. Remove or retire the code (`visor_hud.gd`, the brackets, frame/lens mask). Reuse any useful pure logic, such as timeline progress math, if a minimal element needs it.
- **D-07:** A minimal HUD floats beside the board, as in the sketch:
  - big streak/combo number on the left of the hit ring, caption "STREAK"
  - depth in metres on the right, caption "DEPTH"
  - a thin hull arc under the board (green; red and pulsing when critical, with the "HULL CRITICAL" text)

  Perfect streak, multiplier and the early/late indicator stay available, but small and near the board. Streak milestone popups from 1.1 stay, placed near the streak number. Judgment text appears above the receptors.
- **D-08:** Still allowed: a soft dark vignette at the far screen corners, as atmosphere (no lines or text). Keep it very subtle.

### Arena / world (locked direction)
- **D-09:** The world around the board is a neon abyss arena: a deep gradient with a glowing depth at the vanishing point, a bioluminescent "crowd" (small glowing creatures/silhouettes near the top of the trench) that pulses on the beat and **reacts to good play** (brighter and denser at higher streaks; dims on miss), and marine snow. Optional: a short glitch/flash at the edges on miss, respecting reduce-motion and Low effects.

### Goggles-on intro (locked, sketch 002 winner A)
- **D-10:** Reference: `.planning/sketches/002-goggles-intro/index.html`, variant A. Plays **before every dive**, about 4 s:
  1. a dim real room (desk, monitor glow, "NEURAL DIVE RIG · READY")
  2. goggles slide down and grow until the lenses fill the screen
  3. black
  4. CRT turn-on: dot → horizontal line → full picture with a white flash and "LINK ESTABLISHED"
  5. the arena fades in and the cables grow out of the depth
  6. 3-2-1 on beats, then "DIVE", then the song starts

  The count-in replaces the old 2-beat pre-roll pips, and must keep the Conductor lead-in timing correct.
- **D-11:** **Space skips** the intro straight to a short count-in. (Esc stays pause/back. A gamepad equivalent is A/South.) **Retry and F4-autoplay restart** use the quick version (CRT blink plus a single count, under 1 s).
- **D-12:** Room and goggles art can be stylised vector or _draw art, like the sketch. No external art assets are needed. Pick a sound for the blink and the "DIVE" if cheap (procedural).

### Platform & quality
- **D-13:** Implementation approach (2D projected `_draw` like the sketch vs Godot 3D Camera/meshes) is up to research and Claude. Constraints: Compatibility renderer, **≥60 fps at 1920×1080** on the dev PC, readable at all scroll speeds (0.5×–3.0×), lane brightness/readability not reduced by effects. **Low effects** simplifies the walls (fewer rings, no glitch, fewer particles).
- **D-14:** Keep everything from 1.1: keys 1-4, fullscreen/F11, practice, records, NEW RECORD, IMPLOSION stats, settings overlay from pause, calibration wizard. Calibration screens may keep the simple look.
- **D-15:** End of phase: update the windowed playtest harness (new screenshots: intro frames, trench board far/ready notes, HUD, low effects; timing/autoplay asserts; fps), re-export, update the README ("Space skips the intro", new look), and build `build/dist/UNDERTOW-playtest-v0.2.zip` (.zip only). Then a human visual checkpoint.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact wall geometry, ring count and colours within the sketch look.
- HUD fonts and sizes within UI-SPEC.
- Intro art details and timing ±0.5 s.
- Whether the old flat Highway stays in the code as a fallback.

</decisions>

## Orchestrator resolutions of research open questions (2026-10-08)
- The working directory IS a git repo (the researcher checked from the wrong cwd). Deleted visor files stay recoverable from git history.
- Combo is shown once: StreakCounter is the visible combo. Hud.combo_value is kept for tests/harness but hidden.
- A skipped intro gives a full 3-beat count-in (LEAD_IN_BEATS=3) with no pre-count visuals. Quick (Retry/F4/Restart) is a 1-beat lead-in after the CRT blink.
- Space is the default `fire` key. Skip is handled only while the intro is active, before gameplay input is enabled, so there is no conflict. Pad A skips too.
- approach = 1.5 s / scroll_speed is the starting value. Tune it from the 0.5×/1×/3× screenshots (feel risk: faster screen speed near the ring).

## Amendments after the v0.2 playtest (user, 2026-10-08)
- **D-16 (supersedes the curved-ring part of D-02/D-04 geometry):** "The curved lines are much harder to hit." The board uses a **flat horizontal hit line and straight cables**. The trench world, walls, glow, crowd and HUD stay. Depth stays linear in time. See 01.2-HUMAN-UAT.md G-1.
- **D-17 (supersedes the goggles art in D-10/D-12):** "Remove the goggles entirely, it doesn't look like goggles at all." There is no outside-view goggles drawing. The intro becomes a first-person lens mask closing in (two lens openings seen from inside), then focus pull, CRT blink and arena. No free CC0 goggles asset was found; a bought asset could replace this later. See 01.2-HUMAN-UAT.md G-2.
