> EXTENDS `01.1-UI-SPEC.md`, which extends `01-UI-SPEC.md`. ## Trench Descent Board (D-02..D-05)

### Projection (exact, from R)

```
VP = (960, 396)   HIT_Y = 930   LANE_SPREAD = 210   FAR_SPREAD = 0.045   Z_SCALE = 0.42
f = lane - 1.5 (lanes 0..3 -> -1.5, -0.5, +0.5, +1.5)
d = (note_time - (song_time + visual_offset)) / approach          # LINEAR in time (D-04)
s = 1/(1 + 1.8*d)   smin = 1/(1 + 1.8) = 0.3571   n = (s - smin)/(1 - smin)
x = 960 + f*210*(0.045 + 0.955*n)          # affine in n: every lane path is a straight line
y = 396 + (930-396)*n                       # no sag, no ring curve (D-16)
approach = 1.5 s / scroll_speed        (0.5x: 3.0 s, 1x: 1.5 s, 3x: 0.5 s)
```
- Clamp for drawing only: `d` to `[-0.15, 1.0]` (singularity at -0.238). The line-crossing test uses unclamped `d`.
- Draw notes far to near (descending `d`). No per-frame allocations in `_draw`.

### Fixed reference points (executor and auditor checks, 1px tolerance)

| Point | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Hit points d=0 | lane 1 (645, 930), lane 2 (855, 930), lane 3 (1065, 930), lane 4 (1275, 930). Flat hit line: every lane crosses at the same y = HIT_Y |
| Flat hit line, end points | f = -2.1 and +2.1 at d = 0: (519, 930) and (1401, 930); a single straight segment |
| Cable convergence d=1 | x = 960 + f*9.45, y = 396 (lanes at about 946, 955, 965, 974) |
| Sample, f=+1.5, d=0.5 | about (1053, 537) |
| Sample, f=+1.5, d=0.2 | about (1151, 710) |
| HUD anchors | left f=-2.6: (414, 930), right f=+2.6: (1506, 930) |

Scroll-speed reading aid at 1x (f = 0 line): 0.1 s ahead = y about 841, 0.3 s = 710, 0.75 s = 537, 1.5 s = 396. Screen speed at the line is about 1495/approach px/s (2.8x the mean speed; PERSPECTIVE 1.8 keeps the last 150 px from rushing). `APPROACH_BASE = 1.5` is a tuning start; adjust from the 0.5x / 1x / 3x screenshots, never the judge windows.

### Cables (4 lanes)

| Part | Spec |
|------|------|
| Core | `#0D1424`, 11px, straight polyline from d=1 to d=0 (41 samples kept for the intro grow; collinear) |
| Line | Lane colour x0.50, 2.5px, on top of the core |
| Signal pulse | Once per beat a dot travels from d=1 to d=0 (`d = 1 - beat_phase`): lane colour, radius `(3+3s)*1.2`, alpha `0.5 * beat_phase`. Off in Low effects and under reduce motion |
| Hit line | Straight horizontal segment between the end points above, 4px `#A0FFF0` at alpha `0.55 + 0.4*pulse` (static 0.75 under reduce motion), plus a 12px `#05D9E8` glow stroke at 0.12 alpha (off in Low effects) |
| No plank, no frets, no fills between cables, no flames, no star power (D-05) | There is no lane background, divider or highway body |

### Notes

| Property | Spec |
|----------|------|
| Shape and colour | Same as 1.1 via `NoteShape`: cyan diamond, amber circle, magenta up-triangle, lime square |
| Size by perspective | Radius `r = 36*s + 5`, box = 2r. At the line r = 41 (82px box, matches the 1.1 80px target). Reading guide: 0.1 s ahead r 37, 0.3 s r 32, 0.75 s r 24, horizon r 18 |
| Outline | `2 + 2*s` px (4px at the ring), 25% fill of the (dimmed or bright) lane colour; never reduce whole-note alpha |
| Far (dim) | `abs(note.time - song_time) > 0.070`: lane colour x0.60, no halo, no glow |
| Ready (bright) | `abs(note.time - song_time) <= 0.070` (judge time, not visual time): full lane colour, 6px halo ring at 35% alpha, plus decorative glow strokes (+8px at 12%, +16px at 6%; off in Low effects, halo stays). Switch is instant, no size change |
| Centre rule | Note centre sits exactly on `Trench.hit_point(lane)` when `note.time == song_time + visual_offset` |
| Sustain | Ribbon along the cable sampled at 8 to 12 points, half-width `0.4*r`; fill lane colour 50% alpha (x0.60 when far), 2px full-colour edge when ready, 100% edge plus soft glow near the ring while held; head clamps at d=0 while held; tail clamps at d=1 |
| Missed | Lane colour at 25% alpha with the destructive X glyph, drifts to d=-0.06 and fades to 0 over 0.25 s (no growth past that) |

### Receptors, numbers, lane flashes

- Receptor per lane at `hit_point(lane)`, `NoteShape.draw_outline`, r = 41, 4px stroke, idle lane colour at 70% alpha.
- On `lane_pressed` (hit or stray): radius x1.2 and back over 120 ms ease-out, 100% alpha, 40% fill, 6px halo at 35%. Held sustain keeps 25% fill. Reduce motion: no scale; brightness change still plays.
- Lane number (bound key name from `Settings.bindings`) at `(hit.x, hit.y + 60)` baseline, Label 20, lane colour at 90%. Lane 2 and 3 numbers sit at y 1011; lane 1 and 4 at y 939.
- Lane flash (replaces the rectangular lane flash): glow polyline on the cable for `d in [0, 0.12]`, 8px wide, plus a soft circle at the hit point, lane colour. PERFECT 100% fading over 120 ms plus the expanding Perfect ring (radius 41 to 82). GOOD 50%, 120 ms, no ring. Stray 25%, 80 ms. MISS: destructive flash, X glyph (two 4px lines) at the hit point, "MISS", nudge 8px / 100 ms (off under reduce motion), plus the arena miss glitch.
- Judgment text spawns at `(hit.x, hit.y - 84)`, centred, rises 48px over 500 ms while fading, max 6 simultaneous.

### SKIPPED (Space, pad A/South, or Esc before the count)

- Allowed only while `not began` and the overlay is active; key echo ignored; handled by the overlay node itself; consumed with `set_input_as_handled()`.
- Result: a 0.12 s `#000000` dip (alpha 1 to 0), arena already at full (cables full dim, receptors visible), `Conductor.begin(stream, 3*spb)` immediately, numerals 3-2-1 and DIVE as above, no pre-count visuals. Gives a full 3-beat count-in (1.5 s at 120 bpm). No dip under reduce motion.
- Skipping during the count or after DIVE does nothing. After the conductor has begun, Esc is the normal pause.

### Skip hint placement

"SPACE TO SKIP": Label 20, +2px spacing, `#8A93AD` at alpha 0.7, centred x = 960, baseline y = 1040. Visible 0.3 to 1.10 s and 1.65 to 2.60 s (hidden through the black/CRT beat so it never flashes). Shows "A TO SKIP" when the last input device was a gamepad. Never shown in QUICK or after the count starts. Not interactive.

### Intro audio (optional, default, procedural SFX bus)

Blink at t = 1.25: 60 ms low thump plus a rising 1.2 to 2.4 kHz blip. Count ticks 440 Hz / 60 ms per numeral, DIVE 880 Hz / 120 ms. If cut for time, the intro is still correct. Sonar/creak/filter audio is Phase 5.
