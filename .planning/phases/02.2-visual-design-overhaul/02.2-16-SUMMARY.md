# Phase 02.2 Plan 16: Timing histogram and results entrance Summary

The results screen now shows the timing chart players asked for. The TIMING panel draws the run's recorded hit offsets in 28 bins of 5 ms, from -70 ms (early, left) to +70 ms (late). Bins inside the Perfect window, read from JudgeConfig, are SONAR_PALE; the others are TEXT_MID. The chart also has dashed window guides, a faint window band, a centre line, the "-70 / 0 / +70" and "EARLY / LATE" labels, and a summary line such as "Average 8 ms late". A run with no hits shows "No hits to chart.".

The screen animates in over 0.89 s. The three panels fade and rise in a stagger, the grade pops, then the bars fill and the histogram grows left to right. Any key, pad button or click during the entrance jumps to the final state and is consumed, so it never triggers Retry by accident. Reduce motion uses fades only.

The polish harness proves every route from real dives: Retry Dive, Song Select and Back to Menu. It also checks that the histogram matches the offsets and the grade letter matches RunStats, and that no menu shows behind any results shot.

Gaps fixed (807715f):
1. **EARLY stacked under TIMING.** At the spec's baseline 268, "EARLY" (x 1314) sat 20 px under the "TIMING" caption (x 1312, baseline 248), so the two read as one caption. EARLY and LATE moved to baseline 292, which keeps 8 px clear of the tallest bar top (y 300).
2. **Flat bars.** The bars looked flat and barebones. Each bar now has an 80% body and a 2 px full-colour top cap. `bar_color` stays the token colour.
3. **Target zone hard to see.** The Perfect window was only two dashed hairlines. A 5% SONAR_PALE band between the guides now marks the target zone; this was added in Task 1 after reading the mockup.

Accepted deviations:
- EARLY/LATE are at baseline 292 instead of 268 (reason above).
- The first-record shot's header has no artist or difficulty chip. The test hand chart has no artist or difficulty metadata; real songs show both (see results_new_record).
- The area below the hint (y 900..1080) is empty, as UI-SPEC 8 lays it out.
