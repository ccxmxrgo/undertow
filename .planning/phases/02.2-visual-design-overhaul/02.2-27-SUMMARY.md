# Phase 02.2 Plan 27: Calm gameplay background (gap G2) Summary

Calm is a new still-trench gameplay background next to the existing Living one. Its walls are frozen at an even spacing and use a smoother rock contour. The VP glow keeps a fixed size and a soft brightness breathe of at most 8 %, and the colonies glow steadily. There is no creature and no miss flash, and there is less, slower snow and fish. You pick it in Settings > Display > Background (default Calm). The harness takes same-moment screenshots of both, and over 0.5 s Calm changes about 120x less than Living.

## What Calm looks like vs Living

Both shots are from the same song (Synth Wave April, Expert, autoplay) at song time 20.07 s (Calm) and 20.10 s (Living):
`uat-shots/background_living.png`, `uat-shots/background_calm.png` (full size: `build/playtest/background_01_background_living.png`, `background_03_background_calm.png`).

| Element | Living (unchanged) | Calm |
|---------|--------------------|------|
| Trench walls | 14 jagged rings scrolling toward the player, radius breathes 3.5 % on every beat | Same 14 rings and teal/violet colours, frozen at `CALM_SCROLL_PHASE` 0.0297 (nearest lit ring at r 875 px, one more at 40 % fade). Smoother contour (`CALM_WOBBLE` 0.45), lines at 75 % alpha (`CALM_WALL_ALPHA`) |
| Band fills, vignette | yes | yes, the same (static) |
| VP depth glow | radius 840 + 72 x pulse | fixed 840 px, alpha 1 - 0.08 x (1 - b) |
| Bio colonies | dots 0.25..0.80 alpha, radius 2..4 px, halo floor 0.5 | dots 0.35 + 0.15 b x tier, clamped to 0.50, fixed 2.5 px; halo 0.6 + 0.2 b, fixed size |
| Lantern fish | up to 26, size and alpha pulse, bob | up to 13, 25 % drift speed, no bob, no pulse |
| Marine snow | 80 flakes, sideways wobble | 24 flakes, 40 % fall speed, straight down |
| Distant creature | every 45..75 s | none |
| Miss edge flash | red edges for 0.12 s | none |

b is the beat pulse low-passed with tau 0.25 s, times 2.0, clamped to 0..1 (`CALM_BEAT_TAU`, `CALM_BEAT_GAIN`). The glow alpha changes by at most about 0.005 per 60 fps frame (the test requires 0.02 or less).

Visual review (screenshots read during the plan): the first Calm pass froze Living's jagged contour. Still, it looked like nervous scribbles over the whole screen, and the 0.35..0.50 colonies were so dim the walls lost their glow. The second pass smoothed the contour, softened the lines and raised the steady halo. The result reads as a quiet cave with concentric rock layers, soft glowing spots and a calm depth glow. The board, HUD and song look are the same in both.

Low effects and Reduce motion still apply on top. Calm + Low has 6 rings, 12 colonies and no glow layer. Calm + Reduce freezes the snow and fish, and the brightness breathe stays. Song Select (`menu_mode`) ignores the style.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Calm wall phase leaked into menu_mode / Living**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (GREEN). `test_menu_mode_ignores_background_style` failed.
- **Issue:** `apply_effect_settings()` in `_init` wrote `CALM_SCROLL_PHASE` into `_scroll` before `menu_mode` was set, so a Song Select arena started at a different phase.
- **Fix:** `_scroll` is never overwritten. The drawn phase comes from `scroll_phase()`: the constant under Calm, `_scroll` otherwise. Living only advances `_scroll` while it is the active style.
- **Commit:** ba17f1d

**2. [Rule 2 - Quality] Calm visual review (allowed by the plan: "if Calm looks flat, adjust and re-shoot")**
- Added `CALM_WOBBLE`, `CALM_WALL_ALPHA` and `CALM_COLONY_R`, and set the halo to 0.6 + 0.2 b. Added a test that the Calm band polygons triangulate and that the colonies stay out of the keep-out, the edges and the consoles. Commit 513521a.

**3. [Rule 1 - Layout] Background button size**
- The first button was a 200 x 48 compact button with body text. It pushed the hint off the column and wrapped it. It now has the pill footprint (160 x 40) and the chip font. Commit 6e61491.
