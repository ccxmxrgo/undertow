# Phase 2.2 Plan 07: Menu music and last-dive card Summary

The main menu now loops a random bundled song preview quietly in the background. It plays on a new Preview bus at -12 dB under the music volume. It fades in over 600 ms and loops a 30 s window with a 400 ms cross-fade. It fades out when Song Select starts its own preview, on a dive, on Calibrate and in the calibration wizard. A "Menu music" setting (on by default) turns it off. A last-dive glass card in the bottom-right shows the last song, a difficulty chip, the length and the best depth in gold, or "No record yet.". The harness now checks all of this and saves Full, Low effects and Reduce motion menu screenshots.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 2 - Missing functionality] MenuMusic waits for the library.** At boot the bundled entries arrive a moment after the menu is built, so a plain start() would find nothing and the menu would stay silent. start() now waits for `entries_added`, and stop() cancels the wait.
2. **[Rule 2 - Missing functionality] The last-dive card refreshes on `SongLibrary.entries_added`.** Otherwise the card would stay hidden at boot even though the last song is about to be listed.
3. **[Rule 2 - Pitfall 7] The menu's device-changed prompt "Calibrate Sonar" button also stops the music**, through the same `_calibrate()` path as the menu item. The first-launch redirect does not start the music.
4. **[Rule 1 - Bug] Loop window.** At first a late preview start produced a very short loop. The window now slides back so it keeps 30 s.
5. **Harness additions beyond the plan list:** "last-dive card shows the best depth in gold" (a record is submitted for the bundled song's expert fingerprint in the temp polish record book) and "menu backdrop pulse follows the menu music tempo". `isolate_settings` also calls `MenuMusic.stop(0.0)`, because a "waiting" MenuMusic would otherwise start when the harness rescan lists the bundled songs.
6. **Visual-review fix in abyss_backdrop.gd:** the searchlight pool (see Visual review).
