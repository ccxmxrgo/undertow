# Phase 02.2 Plan 14: Progress track, section banner and keep-out test Summary

The top of the board now has a progress track: a dark scrim, "INVISIBLE · Duran Duran", the current section name in SONAR_PALE, a glowing cyan route with section ticks (reached ones light up) and a playhead diamond, and "-2:22" remaining. Each time the chart reaches a new section, a chamfered glass "SECTION / CHORUS 1" banner slides in above the vanishing point. A new unit test proves that no HUD or cockpit element covers the note path. With this, the cockpit HUD (VIS-04) is complete.

## What was built

- **ProgressTrack** (UI-SPEC 4.8):
  - Scrim: a cached GradientTexture2D, ABYSS_900 at 80% down to y 76, fading out by y 112.
  - Title line: Label 20 TEXT_MID at (480, 48), cut with "..." at 560 px.
  - Section name: right-aligned at x 1440, cut at 360 px.
  - Track: x 480..1440, y 60..64, with 2x10 ticks.
  - Time remaining: TEXT_LO at (1456, 68).
  - All labels have clip_text on and autowrap off (T-02.2-26). It reads only `Conductor.song_time()`.
- **SectionBanner** (UI-SPEC 4.9):
  - Rect (640, 200, 640, 72). Glass panel with CUT_SM corners, brackets top-right and bottom-left. "SECTION" Label 20 SONAR at baseline 224, the name in Heading 40 TEXT_HI at baseline 262.
  - Motion: slides down 24 px and fades in over DUR_BASE (cubic-out), holds 1.6 s, fades out over 0.3 s.
  - Skipped before 0.5 s and within 2 s of the end. No sections means no banner.
  - Reduce motion: fade only, no sweep. Low effects: no panel glow, no sweep. Registered as an effect node.
- **Hud / Gameplay**: Hud creates both. `Gameplay.start()` calls `hud.setup_song(chart, conductor)`, so restarts get them too.
- **test_layout_keepout.gd** (VIS-10):
  - Builds the polygon from `Trench.point(+-2, 1.0 / -0.043)`, grown by 48 px with a mitre join.
  - With both mode chips and the banner visible, checks every visible Control rect and every `keepout_shapes()` polygon.
  - Also checks that the full-screen canvases implement `keepout_shapes()` and that the banner and progress text sit outside the path.
  - A scanner self-check proves an intruding box and a full-screen canvas without shapes are both caught.
- **Harness visual scenario**:
  - Seven new shots on an INVISIBLE Expert autoplay dive.
  - Checks: "accuracy shows --.-% before the first note", "section banner visible at a section change", "HULL CRITICAL caption shown at <= 30", "depth value box did not move", plus "progress track names the current section".

Compared against UI-SPEC 4.8, 4.9, 7.2 and 7.3 and the mockup progress and banner block. Positions, colours and copy match.

Gaps fixed:
1. The arrival sweep was still mid-panel in a still taken 0.3 s after the section start, where it read as a stray vertical bar. It now finishes in 0.26 s.
2. The playhead was a thin bar that got lost. It now has a SONAR_PALE diamond.

Accepted deviations:
- Reached section ticks are SONAR instead of TEXT_LO 60%. This lets the track read as progress; upcoming ticks keep the spec colour.
- The extra polish layers (stroke glow, end caps, caption rules, sweep) are additions the spec did not ask for. They respond to the "looks barebones" feedback, stay outside the note path and keep 4.8 identical in all modes. The sweep is Full only, like other glow effects.
- In the hull shots the accuracy still reads 100.0%. The harness injects misses straight into HullScore, which does not go through `judge.judged`. This is a harness artefact; real misses update the accuracy.
