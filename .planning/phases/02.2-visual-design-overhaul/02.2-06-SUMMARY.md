# Phase 2.2 Plan 06: Living abyss title screen Summary

The main menu is now an animated deep-sea title screen. AbyssBackdrop draws the scene: a gradient with surface glow, five additive light shafts, a scrolling far ridge, swaying kelp, a bobbing submarine with an additive searchlight and lit portholes, near rocks, marine snow, pulsing bio specks and a vignette. Over it sit the UNDERTOW wordmark, the tagline and seven icon buttons at fixed positions. The wordmark is rendered from Oxanium and has a shimmer shader and a SONAR glow. Focus is shown by a gliding FocusMarker, and a cross-fading line under the buttons describes the focused item. Low effects drops the menu particles from 160 to 40. Reduce motion freezes everything except the speck brightness pulse.
