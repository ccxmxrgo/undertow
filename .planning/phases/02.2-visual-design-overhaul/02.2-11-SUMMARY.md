# Phase 2.2 Plan 11: Living trench Summary

The trench behind the board is now layered and alive. Dark rock bands fill the gaps between the wavy wall rings, and each band has a lit lip, so the walls read as layered rock instead of wireframe. 36 clusters of cyan and violet bioluminescent dots sit on the side walls and ceiling. They ride the wall scroll and flash on every beat, stronger at higher streak tiers (gain x1.0 to x1.3), with soft additive halos. The old crowd is now a school of small cyan and pink lantern fish that swims across the top of the trench. At MAX the school stays at least 75% lit. Now and then a leviathan with a faint row of photophores, or a jellyfish with a violet glow, drifts across the far background above the board. Nothing except the walls ever enters the note path. Low effects and Reduce motion have their own paths. With the whole board slice on, Full p95 is 2.53 ms at 1080p (gate 4.35 ms). A full Expert autoplay of Sea Shanty 2 at Full effects judges 471 Perfect, 0 Good and 0 Miss.

## Visual review

Screenshots opened and compared with UI-SPEC 7.7 (arena layers), 7.1 (keep-out), the whole board and the mockup arena section:
- build/playtest/visual_04_gameplay_trench_beat.png (3 runs)
- build/playtest/visual_05_gameplay_trench_creature_leviathan.png (3 runs)
- build/playtest/visual_06_gameplay_trench_creature_jellyfish.png (3 runs)
- build/playtest/songs_01_library_midlist.png (Song Select, menu-mode arena)
- Arena-only renders at a beat peak and off the beat (scratch, leviathan and jellyfish), used to iterate quickly

Gaps found and fixed:
1. **Band fills invisible.** At 0.03 + 0.05 n of ABYSS_600 on the abyss, the bands did not show and the walls still read as a wireframe. Fix: each band is a vertex-colour gradient. The lip at the nearer ring is at 0.06 + 0.16 n and fades to 30% toward the VP, so it reads as layered rock. A faint wide stroke glow under each wall line was also added (Full only, not on Song Select).
2. **Colonies read as tiny specks** and almost vanished between beats. Fix: the halo is about twice the glow_sm size and has an alpha floor of 0.5. The dots keep the spec pulse, so the beat contrast stays (harness 4.0 vs 2.7).
3. **Crowd was a straight row of amber and green blobs.** Fix: it uses lane-1 and lane-3 hues (cyan and pink), as in UI-SPEC 3.1 and the mockup. It is a school spread over y 108..192 with varied sizes, and the fish swim slowly in their facing direction and fade at the ends of the span.
4. **Creature nearly invisible.** It was an ABYSS_800 silhouette at 50% with a 10% rim. Fix: the leviathan has 7 faint twinkling photophores and the jellyfish has a soft violet bell glow. The silhouette, rim and alpha are as specified.
5. **A colony sat behind the "67 M" depth readout.** Fix: colonies avoid the console corners (below y 584, x < 416 / x > 1504).
6. **Song Select outer band too bright** (songs harness: 0.36 to 0.60 > 0.35). Colony halos and the wall glow near the edge caused it. Fix: colonies keep 64 px from the screen edges and the wall glow is off in menu mode.

Confirmed: the board stays the brightest and most saturated thing on screen. Colonies, fish and the creature stay in the outer trench and above the board. Notes, receptors and popups are untouched (the burst and gem checks from plan 10 still pass).

Accepted deviations:
- Band alpha above the spec formula, plus the wall stroke glow (reason: readability and the user's "barebones" complaint).
- The colony halo is about 2x glow_sm and has a floor. Dot radius is 2 + 2 pulse scaled by depth (0.7..1.3). The spec radius applies at mid depth.
- Creature photophores and jellyfish glow are extra details on top of the silhouette spec.
- Lantern fish swim (slow drift) instead of standing in place. Under Reduce motion they are static.
- Walls are still the 01.2 thin wavy polylines (now with bands and glow). Rebuilding them as solid rock meshes is out of scope for this plan.
