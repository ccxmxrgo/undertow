<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Art direction
- **D-01:** Name: "Abyssal Cockpit". The player sits in a submarine cockpit. The highway is the sonar viewport into a bioluminescent trench. Palette: abyss blacks/navies (#03060D → #0A1A2E), sonar teal (#05D9E8) as the UI chrome colour, the 4 locked lane neons (cyan ◆, amber ●, magenta ▲, lime ■) reserved for lanes and notes, reward gold for records/grades, warning red #FF4A3D. The old terminal green (#39FF14) is retired from menus; it may stay as the "Perfect" judgment colour only if the UI-SPEC keeps it.
- **D-02:** Anti-Guitar-Hero guard: no fretboard wood/metal and no flame effects. The look is glass, light, water and sonar.
- **D-03:** Display typeface: research picks one OFL face (candidates: Orbitron, Rajdhani, Chakra Petch, Exo 2, Oxanium), committed with OFL.txt. Share Tech Mono stays for body text and labels. Downloading the font from Google Fonts / the official repo is approved (OFL).

### Rendering approach
- **D-04:** Stay in 2D (`_draw` + `Trench` projection) for the board. Use `canvas_item` shaders for glow/CRT/water shimmer, `GPUParticles2D` or `CPUParticles2D` (whichever is reliable in Compatibility) for snow/bursts, and `WorldEnvironment` glow if it works on Compatibility 2D (research verifies). Otherwise use additive glow sprites. Every effect has a Low-effects path.
- **D-05:** Procedural or in-repo art only (SVG/PNG drawn by the executor, generated textures). No third-party art packs.
- **D-06:** Performance gate: harness frame-time capture. Full effects p95 ≤ 4.35 ms (≥ 230 fps) at 1080p on the dev PC (Ryzen 7 9800X3D; GPU per harness). Low effects is the integrated-GPU path.

### Screens and functionality
- **D-07:** Menu music: a random bundled song's preview loops at -12 dB relative to music volume, with a Settings toggle "Menu music" (default on). It fades out over 400 ms when entering a dive.
- **D-08:** Accuracy % = (Perfect×100 + Good×50) / judged notes, shown with 1 decimal. Grade thresholds: S ≥ 98 (and 0 Miss), A ≥ 93, B ≥ 85, C ≥ 75, else D. Shown in results only (not live).
- **D-09:** Timing histogram on results: 5 ms bins from −70 to +70 ms, early on the left. Needs the per-hit offsets recorded during the run. This is read-only from the judge's output; if the offsets aren't currently kept, add a recorder in the gameplay/UI layer that listens to judgment events. Never touch the judge.
- **D-10:** Section names come from the chart's `[Events]` `section` events (already parsed? research checks ChartData). If absent, there's no banner and the progress bar still shows.
- **D-11:** Album art: `album.png` / `album.jpg` in the song folder, loaded at runtime (Image.load), cached per song, max 512 px. Generated cover fallback, deterministic from the title hash.
- **D-12:** Intro: the UI-SPEC decides between drawing a real room scene or trimming the room segment. Recommended: trim to lens/CRT/link/count, unless a drawn room looks good within the plan budget.
- **D-13:** Transitions: a 300 ms "sonar wipe" (a radial ping expanding) between screens, skippable, and never blocking input.

### Process
- **D-14:** Run `gsd-ui-phase` (auto) to produce `02.2-UI-SPEC.md` before planning. The UI-SPEC becomes the design contract and supersedes the 01.2 / 01.1 / 01 UI-SPECs where they conflict. Build in vertical slices: (1) design system + menu (visible win first), (2) gameplay board + HUD, (3) results + song select, (4) the remaining screens + intro + juice, (5) perf/low-effects pass + UAT.
- **D-15:** Each slice ends with windowed harness screenshots committed to the phase dir (or referenced from build/playtest) for the UAT review.

<post_research_decisions>
## Decisions after research (orchestrator, 2026-10-10, user asleep)

- **D-16:** Gameplay submits the D-08 weighted accuracy and stores a best grade (and the miss count) with each record. Old records show "-" for grade until replayed. Record IDs and fingerprints don't change.
- **D-17:** Perf gate method: capture with vsync off and max_fps = 0 (uncapped). Full effects p95 frame time ≤ 4.35 ms over the 30 s dense passage at 1080p. Also measure the pre-phase baseline in the first plan, and log the display refresh rate with every capture. The SPEC's "≥ 230 fps" intent stands; only the measurement method changes. This will be stated to the user at UAT.
- **D-18:** Bar lines honour time-signature changes. ChartImporter carries `time_sigs` and `sections` into ChartData (no fingerprint change). Bundled charts do have 6–10 sections, so the section banner and progress label are expected on all 5 songs.
- **D-19:** The zip extractor keeps album.png/jpg so zipped songs also get art.
- **D-20:** The results bleed-through fix has two parts: an opaque results/fail backdrop, and the harness frees or hides the current scene before showing results. A scene-tree test asserts no visible MainMenu.
- **D-21:** Oxanium weight is set with `TextServer.name_to_tag("wght")` (integer tag) in the FontVariation. A test asserts the rendered weight (glyph width or advance) differs from the default.
</post_research_decisions>
