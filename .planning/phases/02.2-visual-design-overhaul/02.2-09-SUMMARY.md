# Phase 2.2 Plan 09: Living surface Summary

The highway now moves with the music and the streak. Beat lines (faint) and bar lines (SONAR, with a soft glow) scroll down the board from the tempo map and the time signatures, so a 3/4 chart has a bar every 3 beats. Sustains are glowing tubes: a soft lane-colour halo, a 45% fill that pulses to 75% with the beat while held, thin edges and a pale core line. The lane strips moved into a child Polygon2D under everything else. It carries the new sonar_surface shader: 2 px scanlines, a sonar sweep from the hit line to the far end once per bar with a 400 ms afterglow, a phosphor lift at the outer rails, and lane-coloured lit-glass edges. The surface also brightens toward the receptors, so each lane reads as its own hue. Streak tiers follow the multiplier. x2 and x3 add rail glow. x4 adds a pale 3 px rail core with glowing dashes flowing toward the player, a receptor halo and a 200 ms entry flash. Dropping a tier fades the extras over 180 ms.

## Visual review

Screenshots opened and compared with UI-SPEC 7.4 / 7.6 and the mockup board:
- build/playtest/visual_01_gameplay_x1.png (first run and final run)
- build/playtest/visual_02_gameplay_x4.png (first run, after the dash fix, final run)
- Two 2x crops of the x4 shot (held tube, left rail)
- Offline probe renders of the board alone at x1 and x4 (a throwaway scene, not committed)

Gaps found and fixed:
1. **Amber and lime strips read olive/brown (orchestrator note).** A 7% tint over the blue-black base cannot show amber. Fixed with the receptor light pool (tint rises toward the hit line) and lane-coloured lit-glass edges in the shader. All four strips now read as cyan, amber, magenta and lime, and the surface looks like lit glass panes instead of flat fills.
2. **MAX dashes invisible.** 70% pale strokes 2..5 px wide disappeared on the 100% pale 3 px x4 core. Widened to 9 px (depth-scaled) with a soft glow. They now read as light beads flowing down both rails.
3. **x1 vs x4 hierarchy.** Confirmed: x1 has thin teal rails; x4 has bright pale rails with glow, dashes and a receptor halo. x4 is clearly special.

Accepted deviations:
- Peak strip tint near the receptors goes past the 14% cap of rule 13.2 (up to 26% at x4). The reason is the hue requirement above, and the contrast pairs are proven by a test.
- A narrow saturated lane-colour stroke over the lower part of held tubes is Feedback's legacy 8 px hit-flash line (feedback.gd `_draw_flash`). It is not from this plan. It goes away with the Feedback/burst restyle in plan 10.
- Beat lines stay at the spec's 7% alpha, so they are subtle by design. The bar lines carry the meter visually.
- The tunnel walls being thin polylines (orchestrator note) belong to Arena (plan 11 arena layers). This plan's files do not cover them, so they are not changed here.
