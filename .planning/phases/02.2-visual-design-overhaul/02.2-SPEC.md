# Phase 2.2: Visual Design Overhaul — Specification

**Created:** 2026-10-10
**Ambiguity score:** 0.19 (gate: ≤ 0.20)
**Requirements:** 12 locked (VIS-01..VIS-10 new; AV-01, AV-02, AV-05 pulled forward from Phase 5)

> **Scope of this spec.** This phase makes every screen look designed and feel alive, without changing timing, judgment or scoring. It locks the *what* and the acceptance bar. The *exact* look (colours beyond the locked palette, layouts, animation curves) goes into a UI-SPEC made by `gsd-ui-phase` from this file. The user went to bed after asking for this, so run on these decisions and save open questions for the end-of-phase UAT.

## Goal

UNDERTOW stops looking like "a road and some numbers". Every screen (menu, song select, gameplay, pause, results, fail, settings, calibration) shares one deliberate visual language: a **deep-sea submarine cockpit looking through a neon sonar into the abyss**. The gameplay board becomes rich and readable, and the HUD becomes instruments you read at a glance. Hits, streaks and the music itself visibly drive the scene. It must still never feel like a Guitar Hero clone, and must stay readable and smooth.

## Background

**User feedback, 2026-10-10** (after approving Phase 2.1): *"i feel the design should be a lot better, it's very barebones, just a 'road and some number' wont do it for me. find a way to make it pretty and functional."* Earlier the same day: *"it looks and feel fine. it's just a bit harder"* (difficulty tuning is deferred and is **not** this phase).

Current state, from the harness screenshots in `build/playtest/` (reviewed 2026-10-10):
- **Main menu** (`polish_13_menu_best_line.png`): a green "UNDERTOW" title and seven identical green-outlined terminal buttons on pure black. It has none of the neon-abyss style the gameplay and song select use, so the two looks don't match.
- **Gameplay** (`songs_16_bundled_gameplay.png`, `immersion_16_effects_full.png`): the Trench Descent board (Phase 1.2) is thin 2 px lane lines converging on a horizon. Notes are hollow outline shapes. There is a single hit line and a green arc (hull) under it. The tunnel walls are faint polylines. The HUD is loose text: a big streak number on the left, and "267 M DEPTH / MULTIPLIER x4 / PERFECT STREAK 78" as small text on the right. The judgment text ("PERFECT") is small and dim. There are no hit explosions worth the name, no song progress, no section names, no score/accuracy, and no sense of a cockpit or a submarine.
- **Results** (`polish_10_results_new_record.png`): a plain list of numbers in a column. **Defect:** the main menu ("UNDERTOW" title and its buttons) shows through behind the results text, so the overlay is not opaque or the menu is not hidden.
- **Song select** (`songs_01_library_midlist.png`): the best-looking screen (a sonar panel and difficulty tiles), but there is no album art, the right panel has a lot of empty space, and it is flat.
- **Intro** (`immersion_01_intro_room.png`): the "room" is grey placeholder rectangles.
- Prior locked decisions still in force unless changed here: lane colours/shapes cyan ◆ / amber ● / magenta ▲ / lime ■; "must NOT feel like Guitar Hero"; 1920×1080 base, `canvas_items` stretch; Compatibility renderer; Share Tech Mono; Low effects + Reduce motion settings exist (`src/autoload/settings.gd`).

### Changed prior decisions (user feedback overrides them)
- **01.2 "no shaders, bloom stays Phase 5"** → **shaders and glow are now allowed** (Compatibility renderer supports `canvas_item` shaders and, since 4.3, glow). Each one needs a Low-effects fallback (requirement 11).
- **01.2 "clean-edge rule: nothing persistent in the outer 32 px"** → relaxed. A **cockpit frame** (viewport bezel, instrument clusters in the lower corners) is allowed. The centre ~60% of the screen around the highway stays uncluttered, so notes are always the most readable thing.
- **Single font** → a **second display typeface** is allowed for titles and big numerals, if it is OFL-licensed and committed to `assets/fonts/` with its licence file. Share Tech Mono stays for body text and labels. Pick from OFL faces (e.g. Orbitron, Rajdhani, Chakra Petch, Exo 2, Oxanium). Research picks one and records why.

## Requirements

1. **VIS-01 One design system for all screens**: There is a written design system and a shared implementation that every screen uses.
   - Current: the menu uses green terminal boxes, song select uses teal panels, results uses green text. Each screen hand-draws its own styling.
   - Target: in the UI-SPEC, colour tokens (abyss blacks/blues, sonar teal, the 4 lane neons, a warning red, a reward gold), the type scale (display face + Share Tech Mono), spacing, panel style (dark translucent "glass" panels with a 1–2 px neon edge, corner brackets and a soft glow), button states (idle/focus/pressed/disabled, with an animated focus marker), and motion rules (durations 120–300 ms, ease-out). They are implemented once, as `ui/undertow_theme.tres` plus shared style/helper scripts, and every screen uses them. Focus is always visible and the whole UI is keyboard/gamepad navigable, as now.
   - Acceptance: the UI-SPEC has the token tables. A grep shows no screen script hard-codes a colour that isn't a theme/token constant (a test enumerates the `src/ui` scripts and fails on raw `Color(` literals outside the token file, with a short allow-list). Windowed harness screenshots of menu, song select, settings, pause, results and fail all show the same panel/button style.

2. **VIS-02 Main menu becomes a living title screen**
   - Current: a static green list on black.
   - Target: an animated abyss scene behind the menu (parallax rock/kelp silhouettes, light shafts from above, drifting marine snow and bioluminescent specks, and a submarine silhouette slowly descending or idling with its searchlight). The "UNDERTOW" logo is set in the display face with glow and a subtle underwater shimmer. Menu items are styled buttons with an icon glyph and a one-line description of the focused item. A small "last dive" card shows the last song + best depth if there is one. A bundled song's preview loops quietly as menu music (fades on navigation into song select, respects the volume settings, and can be turned off in Settings).
   - Acceptance: screenshots show the scene, logo and styled menu. Every existing menu action still works (harness scenario). Reduce motion freezes parallax/sub motion. Low effects drops the particle count by ≥ 70%. The menu music plays, can be turned off, and stops when a dive starts.

3. **VIS-03 Gameplay board: rich, readable highway**
   - Current: 2 px lane lines, hollow notes, a faint tunnel.
   - Target (keep the Trench Descent perspective and lane colours/shapes):
     - (a) Lanes are filled translucent strips with soft separators and a neon edge, so the highway reads as a surface, not four wires.
     - (b) Beat and bar lines scroll down the highway (bar lines brighter), from the chart tempo map.
     - (c) Notes are **solid gems**: a filled core in the lane colour, a bright rim, a glow halo, and a slight top-light so they read as objects. Chords visibly pair, and sustains are glowing tubes that pulse while held.
     - (d) Receptors are designed pads that light up and squash on press, even when there is no note.
     - (e) **Hit explosions**: a lane-coloured particle burst plus a shockwave ring on Perfect, a smaller one on Good, and a red crack/fizzle on Miss.
     - (f) Highway intensity grows with the streak: edge glow and particle trail at x2/x3/x4, and a "max multiplier" state that is clearly special.
     - (g) The tunnel/trench background has depth: layered walls, bioluminescent life that **pulses on the beat** (AV-02), drifting particles and an occasional distant creature silhouette.
     - Notes and receptors must stay the highest-contrast elements on screen.
   - Acceptance: windowed harness screenshots at x1 and x4 streak show filled lanes, beat lines, solid gems and a hit burst. A readability check: the contrast ratio of each lane's gem core against the highway behind it is ≥ 4.5:1 (computed from the token colours in a unit test). Autoplay Expert on all 5 bundled songs still gives 0 Good / 0 Miss. Fps stays ≥ 230 at 1080p on the dev PC (harness frame-time capture over a 30 s dense passage, p95 frame time ≤ 4.35 ms at the 240 Hz cap) with Full effects.

4. **VIS-04 HUD as cockpit instruments**
   - Current: floating text for streak, depth, multiplier and perfect streak.
   - Target: a cockpit frame with instrument clusters:
     - **Streak/combo**: a big numeral in the display face, plus a segmented **multiplier ring** (x1..x4) that fills with progress to the next multiplier.
     - **Depth gauge**: a vertical bathymeter with tick marks and a moving marker (depth = score). It shows the best-depth marker for this song/difficulty if a record exists.
     - **Hull integrity**: a segmented arc/gauge around the hit zone that cracks visibly as it drops and flashes red at critical (keeps the HULL CRITICAL caption).
     - **Song progress**: a thin progress track with the song title/artist and the **current section name**. A section banner slides in briefly at each section change, from the chart `[Events]` sections.
     - **Accuracy**: a live accuracy % (Perfect = 100, Good = 50, Miss = 0, over judged notes so far), small, next to depth.
     - The early/late indicator and the judgment popup are redesigned. The judgment pops with scale/fade at the hit line and is clearly readable (Body 28 Bold or larger, full opacity for ≥ 250 ms).
     - NO-FAIL / AUTOPLAY badges become styled chips.
     - Phase 3 will add a torpedo tube/reward slot: leave a reserved, visibly empty slot or socket in the cluster layout, so Phase 3 has a home for it. Draw nothing for it now beyond an empty socket.
   - Acceptance: screenshots at streak 0, 25 and 100, at hull 100% / 40% / critical, and at a section change. A unit test covers the accuracy % formula. HUD numbers update with no layout shift (fixed-width numerals). The timing-core files are unchanged: `git diff --stat 72e21ce -- src/core/{conductor,clock_model,input_judge,judge_config,hull_score}.gd` is empty.

5. **VIS-05 Results screen redesigned (and the bleed-through bug fixed)**
   - Current: a plain list, with the main menu visible behind it (defect).
   - Target: a full, opaque results screen in the design system. It shows:
     - the song title/artist and difficulty
     - a large **grade** (S ≥ 98% accuracy, A ≥ 93, B ≥ 85, C ≥ 75, D otherwise; S only with 0 Miss), animated in
     - accuracy %, depth reached with a NEW RECORD badge and the previous best, max combo
     - Perfect/Good/Miss counts with proportional bars
     - an **early/late timing histogram** (hit offsets in 5 ms bins, −70..+70 ms), so the player sees whether they rush or drag; this is functional, not just pretty
     - final hull
     - buttons: Retry, Song Select, Main Menu
   - Practice / Autoplay runs say "not recorded", as now.
   - Acceptance: no menu node is visible or interactive behind the results (a scene-tree test asserts the menu is hidden or not present, and a screenshot check that the area behind the panel has no menu text). A unit test covers the grade thresholds. The histogram matches the recorded hit offsets in a test with a fake run. All three buttons work in the harness.

6. **VIS-06 Song select upgrade**
   - Current: list + flat sonar panel, no art.
   - Target: album art in the detail panel. Use `album.png`/`album.jpg` from the song folder (Clone Hero convention, as in the user's reference folder); otherwise a generated cover (gradient from a hash of the title in abyss tones, plus the title's initials in the display face). Each difficulty tile shows its note count and a 1–5 intensity meter (from notes/s). There is an audio-reactive visualizer bar strip while the preview plays (spectrum analyzer on the preview bus), and an animated background matching the menu scene. The panel's empty space is used (art + stats + best record + section list/length).
   - Acceptance: a song folder with album.png shows it (fixture test). One without shows the generated cover, which is deterministic per title (test). Screenshots of both. Navigation, filter, sort and rescan behaviour are unchanged (the existing `songs` harness scenario passes).

7. **VIS-07 Pause, fail (implosion), settings and calibration restyled**: the same design system, with an opaque or blurred backdrop over the frozen gameplay, and consistent buttons.
   - Acceptance: screenshots of each. The existing harness scenarios for pause, settings and calibration pass unchanged in behaviour.

8. **VIS-08 Intro polish**: the goggles intro's placeholder "room" rectangles are replaced by a drawn scene (desk, the neural dive rig/goggles, a screen glow), in the same style, or the room segment is removed and the intro starts at the lens/CRT stage. The planner/UI-SPEC picks one and records why. Space-to-skip and the timing of the count-in are unchanged.
   - Acceptance: an intro screenshot has no plain grey placeholder rectangles. The intro timeline tests pass.

9. **VIS-09 Game feel / juice**: transitions between screens (a short dive/wipe, ≤ 400 ms, skippable by input), focus/hover micro-animations, a small screen shake on Miss (off with Reduce motion), a streak milestone burst (10/25/50/100), and UI navigation sounds (move/confirm/back blips at a modest volume on the existing SFX bus). Gameplay hit sounds are unchanged.
   - Acceptance: harness checks that transitions finish within 400 ms and never block input. Reduce motion turns shake and big motion off (test).

10. **VIS-10 Readability and accessibility guardrails**: notes stay readable at scroll speeds 0.5×–3×. Lane shapes stay distinct for colour-blind players. No decoration overlaps the highway's note path. Text meets 4.5:1 contrast on its panel.
    - Acceptance: a unit test checks token contrast pairs. Screenshots at 0.5× and 3× scroll.

11. **AV-05 (pulled forward) Low effects keeps it smooth**: every new shader, glow, particle system and parallax layer has a Low-effects path (fewer particles, no glow/blur, a simpler background), and Reduce motion removes camera/parallax/shake motion.
    - Acceptance: harness frame-time capture with Low effects on the dense passage. A test that each new effect node reads the setting. Screenshots at Full and Low.

12. **AV-01 / AV-02 (pulled forward) Sonar CRT look and beat-pulsing abyss life**: AV-02 is covered by 3(g). For AV-01, a subtle sonar/CRT treatment of the highway (phosphor glow, a slow sweep afterglow, and very light scanlines), tasteful and off in Low effects. It must not blur notes.
    - Acceptance: screenshots at Full and Low. The note edges stay crisp (the gem rim stays ≥ 4.5:1 against its neighbour pixels in the screenshot test or token test).

## Constraints

- **The timing core is untouched.** No change to conductor, clock model, input judge, judge config or hull score (gate above). Visuals read the song time and never drive it.
- **Performance:** ≥ 230 fps p95 at 1080p on the dev PC with Full effects (240 Hz cap). Low effects is designed for integrated GPUs.
- **No new runtime dependencies** beyond Godot built-ins and committed OFL fonts. Art is procedural (`_draw`, shaders, particles, generated textures) or authored in-repo as SVG/PNG by the executor. No downloads of third-party art packs. (A font download from Google Fonts is fine: OFL, commit the licence.)
- **Phase 3 compatibility:** the HUD keeps a socket for the torpedo/reward slot. The highway draw code takes extra note types later (ghost notes, disruptor overlays) without a rewrite: notes are drawn through one note-drawing function keyed by type.
- The existing tests and harness keep passing, with screenshot names updated where layouts change.
