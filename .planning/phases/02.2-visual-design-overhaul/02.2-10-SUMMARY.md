# Phase 2.2 Plan 10: Board juice Summary

Hits now react on the board. A Perfect sprays lane-coloured sparks from the receptor rim and adds a shockwave ring (r 41 -> 120), a white core flash and a lane bloom. A Good gives a smaller spray and ring. A Miss draws a red crack, an X and a red fizzle, and the board (only the board) shakes 6 px for 120 ms. At x3 and x4, pale sparks rise along the outer rails, and held sustains spark at the receptor. Everything near the hit line is clipped to y >= 840, and gems draw above it, so an effect never hides the next note. The judgment text moved to the hit line in Heading 40 Oxanium with a keyline. A chord now gets one popup. The early/late readout is restyled with a SONAR arrow and now lives in Feedback. Hit sounds are unchanged.

## Visual review

Screenshots opened and compared with UI-SPEC 7.5, 7.3 (judgment popup, early/late) and the mockup board:
- build/playtest/visual_04_gameplay_burst_perfect.png (each of the 4 runs)
- build/playtest/visual_05_gameplay_burst_spread.png (added during review; ~0.1 s after the Perfect, 3 runs)
- build/playtest/visual_05_gameplay_miss.png (first run) and visual_06_gameplay_miss.png (later runs)

Gaps found and fixed:
1. **Particles invisible under the flash and too small.** At 40 ms the white flash covered them. At ~0.1 s they were a 30 px cluster inside the pad, so a Perfect looked like "a white dot and a ring". Fix: the soft sprites are drawn larger (bright core about 6 to 12 px), lifetime randomness went from 0.35 to 0.15, and particles spawn on the receptor rim. The Perfect now reads as a spray of cyan sparks around the pad with a wide ring.
2. **Two popups stacked in one lane.** A fading PERFECT sat behind the new MISS at similar strength. Fix: crowd fade (the older popup jumps halfway into its fade).
3. **Rail sparks at x4 invisible** at the first size. They now read as pale beads rising along both rails (larger sprites).

Confirmed: the gems above the clip line keep their full lane colour (harness cos 1.0). The popup sits above the receptor and below the next gems, and stays readable over the bursts thanks to the keyline. The early/late "LATE 0 MS" with its arrow sits centred under the board. On a Miss, the crack, the X and the red MISS read together.

Accepted deviations:
- Particle sprite size is larger than a literal 3..6 px square (soft glow at 4x). The reason is readability and the user's "barebones" complaint. Motion values are as specified.
- The Miss crack keeps the spec's 2 px stroke (plus a faint 6 px glow). It is thin but readable together with the X and the red popup.
- The red tint at the screen edges in the Miss shot is the existing Arena miss glitch, not from this plan.
- The large X remnant below the hit line in the Miss shot is the existing HighwayNotes missed-note remnant (4.15).
