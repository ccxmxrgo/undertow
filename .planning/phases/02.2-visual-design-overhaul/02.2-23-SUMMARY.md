# Phase 02.2 Plan 23: UI navigation blips and chip pop Summary

Menus now make soft procedural navigation sounds on the SFX bus:
- a move blip when a key or stick moves focus
- a confirm blip on accept
- a back blip on cancel

They never sound during a running dive or while the calibration wizard measures. Gameplay hit sounds (SfxPool) are untouched. The MAX chip and the NO-FAIL / AUTOPLAY chips now pop in (1.0 -> 1.06 -> 1.0, back-out, DUR_BASE) like the results NEW RECORD chip. The timing core is unchanged (TIMING GATE OK).

## What was built

### Task 1: UiSounds and the blips (TDD: bb7ca17 test, 7a417f9 feat)
- **`AudioSynth.make_ui_blip(name)`**: four tones per UI-SPEC 10.4. They are phase-continuous with a 2 ms attack and an 8 ms release, so both ends start and end at 0. Unknown names return null.
- **`src/autoload/ui_sounds.gd`** (autoload `UiSounds`, after ScreenTransition, process mode always):
  - a pool of 4 AudioStreamPlayers on `SFX`, with streams built once
  - `play(name)` returns false for unknown names, while muted, and while a Gameplay runs
  - `_input` only observes events and never consumes them: ui_accept plays confirm, ui_cancel plays back, a navigation press arms move
  - the root viewport `gui_focus_changed` plays move only when navigation is armed
- **calibration_wizard.gd**: `show_step` sets `UiSounds.muted` for AUDIO and VISUAL, and `_exit_tree` clears it. The `_input` tap stamping and the click track are unchanged.

### Task 2: Chip pop and harness (478b045)
- **MultiplierRing**: the MAX chip is now a child Control `MaxChip` at the same rect. It pops when x4 is reached from below. Staying at x4 does not re-pop, and losing x4 hides it. `bind()` sets the chip's visibility without a pop.
- **Hud**: the NO-FAIL and AUTOPLAY chips are child Controls placed at `chip_rects()`, which is still the layout and keep-out source. A chip pops when it appears, through `bind` / `_refresh_all` or `set_autoplay`.
- **ResultsScreen**: no change was needed. The NEW RECORD chip already pops at `RECORD_AT = EMBLEM_DELAY + DUR_SLOW`, after the grade pop (plan 15).
- **Harness**:
  - polish checks that the menu Down press (Start -> Practice) plays "move" on SFX.
  - polish checks the first autoplay dive: while the dive runs, accept and move are fed to UiSounds three times, and play_count and last_played must not change.
  - sc_a checks the unscored audio test mid-run: muted, then Tab, then a forced focus change right after a ui_down. Nothing may play. It also checks that UiSounds is unmuted once the wizard has been left.
- **tests/test_hud_gauges.gd**: three new tests:
  - MAX pops once, and hides when x4 is lost
  - no pop under Reduce motion
  - mode chips sit at their rects and scale about their centre (the tween is stepped by hand)

- **Fix:** Hud now keeps `mode_chip_tweens`, and the test pauses the tween and steps it with `custom_step`.
- **Commit:** 478b045

**3. [Rule 2 - Strengthened check] The calibration probe could not move focus**
- **Issue:** The running audio step has one focusable button (Skip), so Tab did not change focus, and the first "no UI blip during calibration measuring" run proved nothing beyond `muted`.
- **Fix:** The check now also feeds a ui_down to UiSounds and forces a real focus change on Skip. Outside the wizard, that would play move.
- **Commit:** 478b045

### Interpretations
- **"move on gui_focus_changed"** fires only after a navigation press, so screens that grab focus on open stay silent.
- **The plan names ResultsScreen**, but its NEW RECORD pop was already done in plan 15, so the file is unchanged.
- **Mouse clicks on buttons do not blip.** The plan wires ui_accept only. UI-SPEC 9 lists a confirm blip on "Pressed", which a mouse click would also need. This is logged as a possible follow-up, not built.
- **Song Select's list moves selection without changing focus** (its root keeps focus), so Up and Down in the song list are silent. Wiring it would mean calling `UiSounds.play("move")` from song_select.gd, which is outside this plan's files. This is a follow-up.
