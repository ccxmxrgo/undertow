# Phase 02.2 Plan 15: Results data and grade emblem Summary

After a dive the player now gets a real results screen. It is opaque and sits on the calm abyss. The header shows DIVE COMPLETE, the title, artist and a difficulty chip, plus NOT RECORDED and flag chips when they apply. A glass grade panel holds a chamfered octagon emblem with the D-08 grade letter: it pops in from 1.6x, and an S gets a gold ring. Below it sits the weighted accuracy. The stats panel shows depth with a NEW RECORD chip and the previous best, max combo and final hull (red at 30% or less), and PERFECT/GOOD/MISS rows with proportional bars. The TIMING panel waits for plan 16's histogram. Three buttons wrap focus in a closed loop: Retry Dive, Song Select and Back to Menu. Underneath, every run records its hit offsets and submits the D-08 accuracy. Each record now keeps a best grade and miss count, and legacy records still load unchanged.

## What was built

### Task 1 (done earlier: 530ed99 test, fdf9674 feat)
- **ScoreStore**:
  - `submit` takes optional `grade` and `misses`. They are stored when the new grade ranks higher, or when nothing is stored yet.
  - `best()` returns `grade` ("" for legacy entries) and `misses` (-1 for legacy).
  - `load()` whitelists grade to S/A/B/C/D and clamps misses to 0..MAX_COMBO or -1 (T-02.2-27).
  - Ids, fingerprints and the depth/accuracy/max_combo/date keys are untouched (T-02.2-28).
- **Gameplay**:
  - `offset_recorder` attaches after the judge exists, resets on restart and detaches in teardown.
  - `_finish` submits `RunStats.accuracy` and `RunStats.grade`.
  - `record_info` gains grade, accuracy and offsets. `run_info` carries title, artist, difficulty, offsets, accuracy, grade and `perfect_window` (read from JudgeConfig).
  - `go_to_song_select()` changes to `res://scenes/song_select.tscn` and keeps the run's `SongSelect.run_autoplay` / `run_practice`.
- **Tests**: grade/misses storage, sanitising, legacy loading, the D-08 submit path, and an autoplay run that records `offsets().size() == perfect + good`.
