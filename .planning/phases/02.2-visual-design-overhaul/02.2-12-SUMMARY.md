# Phase 02.2 Plan 12: Cockpit frame and left console Summary

The board now sits inside a sub cockpit. Left and right consoles start at y 600, and curved bezels frame the top corners. The left console reads like an instrument cluster: a big always-on STREAK numeral, a segmented x1..x4 multiplier ring that fills notch by notch and turns gold with a MAX chip at x4, the perfect streak, and an empty torpedo socket for Phase 3. Each streak milestone pops a label, fires a spark burst and flashes the highway rails.

## What was built

- **CockpitFrame**: draws once and stays cached (`redraw_count` is 1 after 60 frames, processing off). It draws:
  - console polygons per UI-SPEC 7.2 (ABYSS_800 82%, 2 px SONAR 40% on the top, chamfer and inner edges, corner brackets)
  - quadratic top bezels (ABYSS_900 90%, 1 px SONAR 25% inner curve)
  - 2 px SONAR_PALE 8% glass glints

  It also provides `keepout_shapes()`, which returns both consoles and both bezels. It sits between BoardRoot and Hud.
- **MultiplierRing**:
  - 3 arcs x 10 notches (A1 -85..25, A2 35..145, A3 155..265, 1.5 degree gaps), centre (144, 912), r 78..96.
  - Lit notches are SONAR, unlit ABYSS_500 70%. At x4 all notches turn REWARD_GOLD with a glow_md halo behind them (additive child, show_behind_parent), an orbiting 20 degree glint and the MAX reward chip.
  - On a step-up the ring flashes SONAR_PALE for 120 ms and the numeral pops to 1.15. A combo break drains the notches over 180 ms (cubic-out).
  - Effect modes: Low drops the glow and glint. Reduce drops the pop, drain and glint.
- **TorpedoSocket**: recessed ABYSS_900 circle (r 36), 12-dash TEXT_LO 35% ring at r 30, and a SONAR 20% outer ring. No text and no animation, so it is not an effect node. `socket_rect()` returns (272, 928, 80, 80).
- **StreakCounter**:
  - Display 96 Oxanium numeral, always visible, right-aligned at x 368 (baseline 736) in a fixed box at least 222 px wide. Colours: TEXT_LO below 10, TEXT_HI from 10 to 49, SONAR_PALE from 50, plus a text glow from 100 (not on Low).
  - "STREAK" caption at (48, 644).
  - Pops to 1.08 over 80 ms each time the streak crosses a multiple of 10. A break resets it instantly.
  - Milestone popup centred at x 208, baseline 584, with a 3 px KEYLINE outline, back-out pop, 32 px rise and 700 ms fade. A one-shot CPUParticles2D fires from (257, 700): 24 sparks on Full, 8 on Low, 0 under Reduce. It also calls `highway.flash_rails(0.6, 0.16)`.
  - Provides `keepout_shapes()` (numeral box, caption, popup area). Removed from PENDING_MIGRATION.
- **Hud**: MultiplierRing and TorpedoSocket children bound to HullScore (read-only signal listeners). The PERFECT caption (264, 852) and the SONAR_PALE Heading 40 value (baseline 896) are drawn in `_draw`. The old right-column MULTIPLIER/PERFECT STREAK captions are gone. The right side is untouched until plan 13.
