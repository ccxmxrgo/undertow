# Phase 02.2 Plan 24: Low effects / Reduce motion pass, token cleanup and final evidence Summary

**Tests:** one test (`test_effect_settings.gd`) enumerates every effect owner on the menu, song select, gameplay and results screens. It proves that each owner honours Low effects and Reduce motion and restores Full exactly. It checks these UI-SPEC 11 values:
- menu particles 160 -> 40
- gameplay snow 80 -> 24
- bursts 24/10/6 -> 8/4/0
- screen shake goes off only under Reduce motion

**Palette:** the legacy aliases, NOTE_DIM and dim() are deleted, and their last users are migrated.

**Harness:**
- new checks for gem readability at 0.5x / 3x, the gem rim, and Full vs Low on one frame
- autoplay5 now covers all 5 bundled songs, each with 0 Good / 0 Miss

**Perf:** the real perf-gate number still needs the user's PC (software GL in the cloud).
