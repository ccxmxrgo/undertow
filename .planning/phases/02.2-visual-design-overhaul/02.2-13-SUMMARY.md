# Phase 02.2 Plan 13: Right console and hull gauge Summary

The right console now works like an instrument cluster, not a text list:

- a DEPTH readout in Oxanium SONAR, in a fixed box with an "M" unit
- live ACCURACY that shows "--.-%" until the first judged note, then "98.4%"-style values
- NO-FAIL and AUTOPLAY mode chips
- a glass-tube bathymeter whose water column and marker sink with the dive, scaled to an all-Perfect estimate, with a gold BEST line at the saved record

The hull is now a 20-segment arc around the hit zone. As it drops, segments go dark and crack. Below 50% it turns TEXT_HI. At 30% and below it turns red, pulses, every dark segment shows a crack, and HULL CRITICAL appears.

## What was built

- **Bathymeter** (UI-SPEC 4.6):
  - Tube at x 1792..1808, y 632..1032: ABYSS_800 fill, 1 px SONAR 35% outline, round caps.
  - Ticks: minor every 1/20, major every 1/4.
  - Water column in SONAR 35% down to the marker.
  - SONAR marker triangle with its tip at (1790, y). It eases with tau 0.08 s and jumps under Reduce motion.
  - BEST line: 2 px REWARD_GOLD at x 1786..1814 with a "BEST" label at x 1822. It flashes once (DUR_BASE) when the dive passes it.
  - `estimate_max_depth(chart, cfg)` only reads JudgeConfig (T-02.2-25).
- **HullArc** (UI-SPEC 4.7):
  - Geometry kept: centre (960, 450), r 612, band 607..617, angles 1.114..2.028.
  - 20 segments with 0.006 rad gaps. The boundary segment is drawn at partial alpha. Unlit segments are ABYSS_500 60%.
  - Cracks are precomputed per segment index: 2 jagged 3-point polylines at TEXT_HI 60%, 1.5 px. They show on lost segments within 3 of the fill edge, and on every unlit segment at 30% and below.
  - 30% tick: 2x16 px TEXT_HI.
  - 3 Hz pulse while critical, static under Reduce motion. Registered as an effect node.
- **Hud**:
  - Retired: the old 01.2 right column (COL_X/VAL_X labels), the badge drawing (`_draw_badge`), the inline 4 px arc and every colour literal. hud.gd has left PENDING_MIGRATION.
  - New: the DEPTH and ACCURACY captions, the depth box, the accuracy label and the chips. HULL CRITICAL is now a Label 20 bold WARNING_RED caption with a 3 px KEYLINE outline, centred at x 960 on baseline 1028.
  - `keepout_shapes()` returns the captions, readouts and chips, without the allow-listed arc and caption.
  - Kept: the hidden combo/hull/depth/mult/streak labels and the existing Hud statics (`hull_arc_angle`, `hull_fill_alpha`, `critical_pulsing`, `is_critical`, `depth_text`).
- **Gameplay**:
  - Connects `judge.judged -> hud.on_judged` after HullScore.
  - Sets the bathymeter scale from `Bathymeter.estimate_max_depth(chart, cfg)`.
  - Passes `ScoreStore.best(chart.id)` depth, or -1, through `hud.set_record`. Restart goes through `start()`, so it is refreshed too.
