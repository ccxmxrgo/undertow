# Phase 02.2.1.1 Plan 02: Fade-in notes and calmer far end Summary

Gems, chord bars, halos and sustains now fade in over the far end by Trench.note_fade, while strips, rails, separators, beat/bar lines, MAX dashes and the shader sweep/phosphor fade out by Trench.line_fade; all draw-only and depth-based.

## Accomplishments
- NotePainter: optional trailing `alpha := 1.0` on draw_note, _draw_normal, draw_chord_bar and draw_halo, plus `faded()` helper (alpha 1.0 returns colour untouched; missed remnants keep their own fade and are not depth-faded).
- Highway: note_fade per gem / chord bar / halo (skipped under 0.01); sustain tubes get per-quad alpha (body note_fade, glow line_fade) and per-sample polyline colours for edges and core; rails, separators and rail glows/flash/MAX core go through `_draw_fading_line` (draw_polyline_colors, member buffers, no per-frame allocation); beat/bar lines and MAX dashes scale alpha by line_fade; strip_color multiplies by line_fade; STRIP_ROW_D gained rows 0.85 and 0.6.
- Shader: `fade_start` uniform (set from Trench.LINE_FADE_START) fades the sweep band/trail and phosphor; scanlines untouched; no colour constants.
- No effect_counts() key changes; fades not gated on Low effects / Reduce motion.
- Tests: faded() unit test, painter draw with alpha, strip_color alpha at HIT_Y vs far end.
