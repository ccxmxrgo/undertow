  <action>
    Rework the visor into a convincing diver-goggle HUD. Keep everything within the Compatibility renderer and keep the shader mask/alpha-based, so there is NO screen-texture warp of the highway.

    1. **Lens shape:** the shader draws an opaque near-black goggle mask outside a superellipse lens opening. Use exponent about 4, a rounded rectangle with curved sides, about 88% of the width and 92% of the height. This makes the screen edges look curved. Add a 6–10px inner rim line in sonar green with a soft glow (smoothstep falloff) along the lens edge, plus a faint second bevel line 12px further in.
    2. **Vignette:** a heavy dark gradient from the lens edge inward, reaching about 0.8 alpha at the rim. It MUST be exactly 0 inside the highway rect (x 672..1248) and its 48px margin, enforced in the shader with a uniform `clear_rect`.
    3. **Scanlines:** at 540 lines and about 0.08 alpha, outside the clear_rect only. Add a slow horizontal "sweep" band (8s period, about 0.06 alpha), also outside clear_rect. Add a faint cyan/magenta chromatic tint (2px) on the rim line only.
    4. **Instrument HUD** in a new `src/ui/visor_hud.gd` (_draw), drawn in the frame above the vignette and below pause/results overlays. Readouts are bound to real values where available:
       - a top heading/compass strip with tick marks, centred above the highway, not overlapping it
       - left and right vertical tick rulers along the lens edge, with the right ruler a depth gauge whose marker moves with depth
       - a bottom status strip: "O2 98%" (decorative slow drain), "HULL {hull}%", a blinking "● REC"
       - reticle corner marks replacing or augmenting `VisorBrackets`
       Use sonar-green chrome text in the UI-SPEC sizes (20px label), and red only when the hull is critical.
    5. Move "UNDERTOW // VISOR" into the top-left rim area (inside the lens, outside the highway). Make sure no visor text overlaps x 672..1248 for y < 120.
    6. **Low effects:** keep the lens mask, rim and static HUD. Drop the sweep, scanlines, chromatic tint and blinking.
    7. **Performance:** add no per-frame allocations in _draw. Batch the ruler ticks.
    8. Update the 01.1-UI-SPEC visor section to the new design (one short subsection) and note "AV-01 visor portion pulled forward from Phase 5 per user, 2026-10-08".
  </action>
