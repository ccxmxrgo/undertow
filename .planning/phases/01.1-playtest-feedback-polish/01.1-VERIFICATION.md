### Bug found and fixed by the harness

`window/size/mode=3` (window created fullscreen) made F11 / Settings Fullscreen OFF a no-op on Godot 4.7.2 Windows: `window_set_mode(WINDOWED)` reported EXCLUSIVE_FULLSCREEN (4) and ignored the 1600x900 size. Reproduced in a minimal standalone project. Fix (a86457e): the window is created windowed at 1600x900 and `Settings.apply_window()` switches to fullscreen at boot. Regression test `test_project_creates_window_windowed_1600x900`. After the fix: windowed mode 0, 1600x900, centered at (2080, 90) on the primary screen; F11 off/on both pass.
