Inherited unchanged (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64). New exceptions for this phase:

- Receptor and note glyph bounding box: 80px (unchanged), centered in lane. Receptor center y = 880 (the hull line).
- In-window note halo: +6px beyond the outline (stroke exception, not spacing).
- Visor bracket inset: **16px (md)** from each screen edge; bracket leg length **64px (3xl)**; stroke 2px. Frame chrome (brackets, edge readouts) is the ONLY content allowed inside the 32px HUD safe margin. It is non-interactive and must not overlap any HUD value.

- Windowed mode is 1600x900 centered; layout is unchanged because the canvas stretches (D-06).

## Color

Dominant/secondary/accent split is unchanged (60% `#000000`, 30% `#0A1F0A` / `#1F8F0C` / `#8FD98A`, 10% accent `#39FF14`, destructive `#FF4A3D`). Lane colors are an additive, lane-scoped palette; they occupy only gameplay glyph pixels, so they sit within the 10% budget.

### Lane colors (D-02), constants in `Palette.LANE_COLORS`

| Lane | Shape | Hex | Dimmed (x0.60 RGB, "far" state) | Contrast bright on `#000` | Contrast dimmed on `#000` |
|------|-------|-----|------|------|------|
| 1 | Diamond | `#22D3EE` cyan | `#14 7F 8F` (approx) | 11.6:1 | approx 4.6:1 |
| 2 | Circle | `#FBBF24` amber | `#96 72 16` (approx) | 12.6:1 | approx 4.9:1 |
| 3 | Triangle (up) | `#F472B6` magenta | `#92 44 6D` (approx) | 7.9:1 | approx 3.4:1 |
| 4 | Square | `#A3E635` lime | `#62 8A 20` (approx) | 13.9:1 | approx 5.3:1 |

Contrast was computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula (bright values) and estimated for the dimmed values. All bright values exceed 4.5:1 (text) and 3:1 (graphics) on `#000000`, and exceed 6.5:1 on the `#0A1F0A` panel. Every dimmed value stays at or above 3:1 (graphic threshold), so far notes remain visible. Verify the dimmed values once with a playtest screenshot (default: if magenta reads too faint, raise only the dim factor, not the hue).

**Dim factor:** D-05 says "about 55%". The contract uses **0.60** uniformly so the weakest hue (magenta) stays above 3:1 on black. This is the researcher's refinement of "about 55%"; the Palette constant `NOTE_DIM = 0.60` is the single tunable. Dimming scales RGB and keeps alpha at 1.0 (never reduce alpha: the 25% fill would let lane dividers show through).

Color-vision safety: amber and lime can converge for some players. Shape (diamond/circle/triangle/square), lane position, and the lane-colored key label are the redundant channels (Phase 1 D-22/D-24 still holds). Color is never the sole signal.

### Lane colors are reserved for (closed list)

1. Note outline and 25% fill (dimmed when far, bright when in window)
2. Sustain bar and tail
3. Hit receptor outline and press pulse
4. Lane hit flash (full lane for Perfect, half brightness for Good, 25% for stray press)
5. Perfect ring
6. Lane key label under the receptor
7. Miss note remnant (the missed note at 25% alpha, in its own lane color)

Lane colors are NEVER used for: HUD text, menus, buttons, focus, hull bar, judgment text, popups, the visor frame.

### Accent `#39FF14` reserved for (replaces the Phase 1 list)

1. The hull line and lane outer edges
2. The single focus indicator (border + caret)
3. PERFECT floating text and the Perfect streak value
4. Display values (countdown numerals, calibration result)
5. Hull bar fill while hull is above 30
6. Big streak counter number from streak 10 upward, and milestone popups (lane-neutral per D-08)
7. "NEW RECORD" on the results screen

Streak counter number below 10 uses `#8FD98A`.

### Destructive `#FF4A3D` reserved for

Unchanged from Phase 1 (hull bar at 30 or below, MISS text and X glyph, IMPLOSION, destructive confirmations and rebind errors, Bluetooth warning). Phase 1.1 adds: the "NOT RECORDED" line is NOT destructive (secondary text `#8FD98A`). Red never appears in the visor frame.

### Visor frame colors

Superseded by 01.1-06: see "Chrome palette" in the Visor frame section (`#E8E6F0` text, `#FF1F4B` accents, `#05D9E8` data). The visor never uses the destructive `#FF4A3D` except the HULL readout while critical. Lane colors appear only on the three decorative layer chips (user request).

## Gameplay Visual Contract (new and changed)

Draw order, back to front: background, lane fills and dividers, sustain bars, notes, receptors, hull line, Feedback (flashes, rings, floating text), **visor shader rect**, HUD and streak column, **visor brackets and readouts**, pause/fail/results overlays. The visor shader sits below the HUD so HUD text is never darkened (keeps the Phase 1 HUD contrast). Overlays (pause, countdown, IMPLOSION, results) are above the visor. Both visor nodes use `mouse_filter = IGNORE` and the `UiLayout.fill_rect` pattern.

### Notes: far versus ready (D-05)

| State | Condition | Look |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Far (pending, outside window) | `abs(note.time - song_time) > good_window` (0.070 s) | Lane color x0.60, 4px outline, 25% fill, no halo |
| Ready (pending, inside Good window) | `abs(note.time - song_time) <= good_window` | Full lane color, 4px outline, 25% fill, plus a 6px outer halo ring at 35% alpha in the same hue |
| Hit | judged | Removed (flash and ring take over) |
| Missed | marked missed | Lane color at 25% alpha, falls through (Phase 1 behavior) |

- Ready-state uses **judge time** (song_time), not visual-offset time, so brightening matches what the judge will accept. Position still uses visual time.
- The switch is instant (no tween): the on/off edge is the signal. No size change (a size change would move the apparent center and confuse timing).
- The note CENTER marks the hit time: the receptor's center sits on y = 880 and the note shape's centroid (center of its 80px box) crosses it at t = 0. Triangle uses box-center, not centroid-of-area, for consistency with the receptor (same `NoteShape` call, so they overlay exactly).
- Sustain bar: dimmed (x0.60 at 50% alpha) until its head is ready or held; bright (full color at 50% alpha, 2px full-color edge) when ready; while held, full color at 100% edge plus a thin lane-color glow along the receptor.

### Receptor (D-04)

- One per lane, drawn by `NoteShape` with the same shape enum and the same 80px box as the note, at (lane center x, y = 880), **outline only** (no fill), 4px stroke.
- Idle: lane color at 70% alpha. This must read as a "target" without competing with in-window notes (which are 100% plus halo).
- Pressed (on `lane_pressed`, regardless of hit or stray): scale 1.0 to 1.2 and back over 120 ms (ease-out), outline 100% alpha, fill 40% lane color for the duration, plus the 6px halo at 35% alpha. While the key is held (sustain), the fill stays at 25%.
- Reduce motion: no scale; the brightness/fill change still plays (state change, not movement).
- A note is "on target" when its outline overlaps the receptor outline exactly; at the moment of overlap the note is inside the Good window by construction. This is the intended answer to feedback item 1 ("highlighted but not ready").

### Hit feedback in lane color (D-02)

| Judgment | Change from Phase 1 |
|----------|--------------------|
| PERFECT | Lane flash and expanding ring (80px to 160px) in the lane color. "PERFECT" text stays accent |
| GOOD | Half-brightness lane flash in the lane color, no ring. "GOOD" text stays `#8FD98A` |
| MISS | Unchanged: destructive flash, X glyph, "MISS" in `#FF4A3D`, nudge |
| Stray press | 25% lane-color flash, 80 ms (receptor still pulses) |

### Big streak counter and milestone popups (D-08, D-13)

Counter = **combo** (consecutive Perfect+Good; resets on Miss or stray press). The Perfect streak stays in the HUD right column as the smaller, torpedo-bound counter (Heading size, accent value, caption "PERFECT STREAK"); the HUD COMBO row also stays (D-08).

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Position | Streak column: number right-aligned at x = 672, vertical center at y = 760; caption "NOTE STREAK" (Label) 8px above the number's top |
| Visibility | Hidden below 5. Appears at 5 with a fade-in of 120 ms (no fade under reduce motion) |
| Tier 5 to 9 | Heading 40px, `#8FD98A`, no glow |
| Tier 10 to 49 | Display 96px, `#39FF14`, no glow |
| Tier 50 and up | Display 96px, `#39FF14`, glow: text outline 6px in `#39FF14` at 30% alpha |
| Tier 100 and up | Same plus a 2px `#39FF14` underline bar 8px below the number, width = number width (shape cue for the top tier) |
| Increment pulse | Scale 1.0 to 1.1 over 80 ms ease-out (same as HUD values). Off under reduce motion |
| Break | On combo reset the counter disappears instantly (no fade), so loss reads as an immediate event |

Milestone popups at 10, 25, 50, 100, then every 100:
- Text "{n} STREAK" (Heading, UPPERCASE, `#39FF14`), centered in the streak column at x = 384, y = 640 (above the counter, off the lanes).
- Motion: rises 32px over 700 ms while fading from 100% to 0% (ease-out). Under reduce motion: no rise, static 700 ms then fade-out over 200 ms.
- Max one popup visible; a new milestone replaces the current one. No audio change in this phase (default).
- Does not stack with lane flashes (different region). Does not block input.

### Visor frame (D-03), "inside the diver's goggles" (rev. 01.1-06)

> **AV-01 visor portion pulled forward from Phase 5 per user, 2026-10-08.** Same day, the user set the art direction: take inspiration from the recording-editor ("braindance") look in Cyberpunk 2077, as if you are inside a recording. Inspiration only: no CDPR names, logos or assets. Our own copy only ("DIVE REC").
> The section below supersedes the original static visor (vignette + 64px corners + three edge readouts).

Three nodes. `VisorFrame` (shader rect) sits above Feedback. `VisorHud` (`_draw` instruments) sits above the lens and below the HUD. `VisorBrackets` sits above the streak column. Draw order: ... Feedback, **VisorFrame**, **VisorHud**, HUD, streak column, **VisorBrackets**, pause/fail/results overlays. All three use `mouse_filter = IGNORE`.

**Hard rule:** the shader is mask/alpha only. It has no `SCREEN_TEXTURE`, no `hint_screen_texture` and no BackBufferCopy, so it never warps the highway. It is exactly 0 inside `clear_rect` = x 624..1296 (the highway x 672..1248 plus a 48px margin), full height, and fades back in over the next 48px. Harness proof: with the run frozen, the highway pixels are identical with Low effects on and off.
