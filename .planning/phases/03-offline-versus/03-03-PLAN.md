- `report_status(seat, hull, depth, accuracy, max_streak)`: store on PlayerState (owners call it whenever their hull or score changes).
- `request_fire(seat, t) -> int`: nothing held or wreck -> FIRE_NOTHING (emit FIRE_REFUSED "nothing"); a launch already pending for this seat -> FIRE_PENDING; buff -> apply now at t: DECOY sets decoy_until = time_after_bars(t, decoy_bars), OVERCLOCK sets overclock_until likewise, BALLAST emits amount = cfg.ballast_restore_ratio * jcfg.hull_start; meter.use(); BUFF_APPLIED.
