**LeaveOverlay** (class_name, extends Control):
- Rect Rect2(640,120,640,152), danger chrome at 92%, CUT_SM.
- Title "LEAVE MATCH?".
- Clients get the ping readout "PING {n} MS" and the body "The song keeps playing. Leaving makes your sub a wreck." (TEXT_MID). The host gets "You are the host. Leaving ends the match for everyone." (WARNING_RED).
- Buttons are 48 tall: "Leave Match" (destructive, x 656..952) and "Stay" (default, x 968..1264). Initial focus is on Stay.
- API: `open(is_host: bool, ping_ms: int)`, `close()`, `is_open()`. Signals `leave_confirmed` and `stayed`.
- Input while open, handled in `_input` so it wins over gameplay:
  - ui_left/ui_right move focus.
  - ui_accept activates the focused button.
  - ui_cancel or the `pause` action means Stay.
  - Mark those events handled. Never consume lane actions or the target-cycle action.
- In/out: fade plus slide 16 px over DUR_FAST. Reduce motion: fade only.
- It never touches the conductor or get_tree().paused.
- `debug_state()` returns {open, focus, body, body_role}.

Both widgets call UiKit.register_fx and implement apply_effect_settings and effect_counts.
