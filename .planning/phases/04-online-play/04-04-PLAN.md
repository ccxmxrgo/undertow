  <behavior>
    - `SonarTextField.new()` with `setup(config: String, value: String)` accepts config "name", "port" or "address".
    - Each config applies the § 4.1 charset, case and max length. Typed or pasted characters outside the charset are not inserted: `rejected_count` increments, an error blip is requested and the edge flashes red for 200 ms.
    - The name config upper-cases. The port config takes digits only, max 5.
    - Commit validation:
      - name: trim and collapse spaces; empty reverts to the previous value.
      - port: 1024..65535, else revert and emit `invalid("port_range")`.
      - address: trim; empty is allowed.
    - Signals: `committed(value)`, `cancelled`, `invalid(code)`, `editing_changed(on)`.
    - When focused and not editing, ui_accept starts editing with the caret at the end. While editing, Enter commits and Esc reverts and stops editing. The Esc event is consumed, so the screen never sees ui_cancel while editing.
    - Gamepad while editing:
      - D-pad up/down cycles the character at the caret through the config charset order.
      - JOY_BUTTON_X deletes the character before the caret.
      - D-pad right at the end appends "A" (name), "0" (port) or "." (address) when below max.
    - Ctrl+V pastes filtered clipboard text, truncated to max (clipboard reading is injectable for tests: `var clipboard_fn: Callable`).
    - The caret blinks at a 1.0 s period on Full and Low, and stays steady under Reduce motion. The editing glow is shown on Full only.
    - `effect_counts()` reports {caret_blink, glow}. `debug_state()` returns {config, value, editing, caret, rejected_count, edge_color_role}.
  </behavior>
