## Goal

Charts follow the music. Every note sits on a sound you can hear in the song, at the moment you hear it, and the lane movement follows the tune going up and down. This is how a human Clone Hero charter works. The pipeline that does this is used first to bundle the user's three songs (HRT, Sea Shanty 2, INVISIBLE), then to re-chart the two existing bundled songs. Later the same tool is offered to players for their own audio (STEM-02..05).

## Background (why the current state is wrong)

**User feedback, 2026-10-09** (Phase 2 UAT item c, "do the notes feel in time with the music?"): *"i need them to be playable and to be synced in-game. not like the current state."* → **FAILED.**

Root cause, from `tools/charting/README.txt`: the two bundled charts come from `author_chart.gd`, which reads a hand-written per-bar plan (`section_plans.gd`). **The audio is never analysed.** Notes are 8ths/16ths and lane patterns (stairs/zig/trill) on a fixed 120 BPM grid. Only the global `delay` was fitted to the audio. So the grid lines up with the beat, but the notes don't match anything the player hears: no melody, no rests where the music rests, no accents where the music accents. That is what feels "not synced".

How real Clone Hero charts are made (the target to imitate):
1. **Tempo map first.** The charter beat-maps the song: `[SyncTrack]` `B` (BPM×1000) and `TS` events make the chart grid follow the audio's real beats, including drift and tempo changes. Resolution is 192 ticks per quarter note.
2. **Notes = audible events of one instrument** (usually lead guitar). Each note is placed on the grid at the onset of a real note. Silence in the instrument = no notes.
3. **Lanes follow relative pitch (contour), not absolute pitch.** Higher note → lane further right. The same pitch repeated → same lane. A step up or down → move one lane. Large leaps → move further. When the melody runs out of room, the window shifts. Power chords and accented hits become 2-note chords.
4. **Sustains** for notes that ring (in the chart: the length field of `N lane len`).
5. **Difficulty reductions** from Expert: Hard drops the fastest notes and simplifies chords; Medium is about 8ths with no 3-note chords; Easy is quarter notes and big rhythmic hits on fewer lanes.
6. **Sections and solo markers** in `[Events]` (`section X`, `solo`/`soloend`).

   - Target: `02.1-RESEARCH.md` picks and justifies, with numbers measured on **these three songs**: (a) a stem separator (e.g. Demucs / htdemucs_ft, MDX/UVR, Spleeter), (b) a beat/downbeat tracker for the tempo map (e.g. madmom, BeatNet, beat_this, librosa), (c) onset detection and note transcription on the stem (e.g. Basic Pitch, librosa pyin + onset, CREPE), and (d) any existing auto-charter or rhythm-game chart generator worth borrowing ideas from (Clone Hero / Moonscraper community tools, Dance Dance Convolution, osu! / Beat Saber mappers, YARG tools). Measured facts on this PC: Windows 11, **no NVIDIA GPU detected** (`nvidia-smi` missing), so CPU time must be reported. System Python is **3.14**, and many audio/ML wheels lag on it, so use an isolated venv with a supported Python (e.g. via `uv`). No ffmpeg on PATH.
   - Acceptance: RESEARCH.md has a table per song: separation time on CPU, tempo map result, and the onset-precision numbers from requirement 5 for at least 2 candidate pipelines. It names the chosen pipeline.

2. **Tempo map from the audio**: Each generated chart's `[SyncTrack]` follows the song's real beats.
   - Current: one fixed `B 120000`, and only `delay` is fitted.
   - Target: the beat tracker's beat times become `B` events, **one `B` event on every beat, like the Neversoft reference** (beat-mapping the recording, so the grid follows tempo drift). A song whose tracked beats stay within 2 ms of a constant grid may use a single `B`. `TS` comes from downbeat tracking (default 4/4). Bar 1 is aligned to the first downbeat. The audio lead-in is handled with `delay` or by tempo events before bar 1 (the charter picks). Resolution 192.
   - Acceptance: For each song, the chart's beat ticks converted to seconds (using the game's own tick→time conversion, delay included) are within **±10 ms median, ±20 ms 90th percentile** of the tracker's beats over the whole song. The check runs on the game's decoded audio (requirement 5).

3. **Notes come from audible events (STEM-03, sharpened)**: Expert notes are the onsets of one chosen instrument stem, snapped to the tempo grid.
   - Current: the plan-authored patterns ignore the audio.
   - Target: Detect onsets/notes on the chosen stem, then snap to the nearest of 1/16 or 1/12 (triplet) grid (the closer one wins per note). Snap only when the snap moves the note by ≤ 15 ms; otherwise keep the exact tick. Where the stem is silent (below a threshold relative to its own loudness), there are no notes. Expert density is capped at **8 notes/s over any 1 s window, about 3–5 notes/s average** (Neversoft reference: 4.19 avg, 8 max on a fast metal song) by dropping the weakest onsets. Notes closer than 1/16 at the local tempo are merged.
   - Per-song instrument (default, research may override with a reason): **Sea Shanty 2** → the lead melody (it's a clean MIDI-style tune; this is the showcase that the lanes follow the melody). **INVISIBLE** → the lead guitar/synth line in "other". If it's too sparse, use bass in the verses and lead in the choruses, switched at section boundaries. **HRT** → research picks the most prominent melodic stem after listening/measuring, and records why.
   - Acceptance: requirement 5 metrics pass, and the UAT ear check: every Expert note has a sound under it.

4. **Lanes follow the melody (Clone Hero contour rule)**: Lane choice comes from relative pitch.
   - Current: lane families (stairs/zig/trill/pulse) chosen by the plan.
   - Target: Use the detected pitch of each note (for unpitched/percussive stems, use spectral centroid as "pitch"). The same pitch as the previous note → same lane. Up/down by a step → lane +1/−1. A leap of ≥ 5 semitones → ±2 lanes. The lane is clamped to 0..3, and when the melody keeps climbing past lane 3 (or falling past 0) the mapping window slides so the contour stays readable. Accented hits (top 10% onset strength) on Expert/Hard become 2-note chords with an adjacent lane. No 3+ note chords below Expert. A ringing note longer than 1 beat (detected offset) becomes a sustain, shortened by 1/16 so it never overlaps the next note.
   - Acceptance: unit tests on a synthetic note list: rising scale → lanes 0,1,2,3 then slide. Repeated pitch → same lane. Octave leap → ±2 lanes. Sustain shorter than the gap to the next note. On Sea Shanty 2 Expert, at least 70% of melody direction changes (up vs down between consecutive notes with different pitch) match the lane direction.

5. **Sync is measured, not guessed**: Each bundled song passes a measured sync gate on the game's decoded audio.
   - Current: Phase 2 checked only the grid offset (`grid_offset.py`) of plan-authored charts.
   - Target: Extend the `align` harness scenario / `tools/charting` scripts. For each chart, compare every Expert note's time (game's tick→time + `delay`) with the nearest onset detected on the **charted stem** and on the **decoded mix** (`build/playtest/align_<id>_decoded.wav`). Report median, IQR and the % of notes within ±25 ms. **Calibrate the gate on the reference:** run the same measurement on the Neversoft chart against its `guitar.mp3` stem and `song.mp3`, and record it as the "professional chart" baseline. Our charts must meet the fixed numbers below or be at least as tight as that baseline, whichever is looser. Record both. The global `delay` is fitted to this median.
   - Acceptance (per song, Expert): median note-to-onset error **within ±5 ms**, **≥ 90%** of notes within ±25 ms of a stem onset, **≥ 80%** within ±25 ms of a mix onset. (Perfect window is ±35 ms per `judge_config.gd`.) A live-recorded cross-check (`AudioEffectRecord`) agrees with the decoded stream within 3 ms. The numbers go into `tools/charting/README.txt` and the phase UAT file.

6. **Difficulty reductions**: Hard/Medium/Easy come from Expert by Clone Hero conventions.
   - Target: Targets come from the Neversoft reference table. **Hard**: keeps about 90–100% of Expert note times, chords reduced (3-note → 2-note; about half of Expert's chords become single notes), ≤ 7 notes/s in any 1 s. **Medium**: keeps about 65–75% of Expert times, ≤ 1/8 grid (drop 16ths), ≤ 3% chords (2-note only, strongest accents), ≤ 5 notes/s in any 1 s. **Easy**: keeps about 45–55% of Expert times, mostly 1/4 and dotted rhythms on strong beats and phrase starts, **lanes 0..2 only** (contour remapped onto 3 lanes), no chords, ≤ 4 notes/s in any 1 s, sustains kept (about 15–20% of notes). The lane-step distribution on every difficulty is close to the reference (repeat 20–30%, ±1 40–60%, ±2 15–30%, ±3 ≤ 5%). Every kept note keeps its exact Expert tick, so a reduction never moves a note off the music. Contour direction is kept as far as possible.
   - Acceptance: unit tests check density caps, the allowed grid per difficulty, and that every reduced note's tick exists in Expert.
