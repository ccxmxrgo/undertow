## Task 1: instrument_lanes.py

- `instrument_expert` works in these steps:
  1. It normalises strength per stem by that stem's own 95th percentile.
  2. For each lane it runs `quantize.build_expert` without a density cut. This reuses the 1/16 merge and the 1/16 or 1/12 snap (≤ 15 ms).
  3. Notes on the same tick become one chord of the two strongest lanes. Distinct ticks closer than 1/16 merge into the stronger note.
  4. It ranks by strength to `round(target_nps * span)` notes, then applies the 1 s window cap (`quantize._cap`).
  5. Sustains follow SPEC req 4, bounded by the next tick in the same lane.
- `easy_lane_map` drops the lane with the fewest gems (ties drop the higher lane) and shifts the higher lanes down.
- 9 tests pass. The module imports no numpy.
