## Task 1: fill.py

- `fill_silent(primary, fill, tm, mode="bars"|"sections", section_starts_tick=None, min_bars=1, fill_src="")`
  - In bars mode, each run of at least `min_bars` bars with no primary onset takes the fill onsets in those bars.
  - In sections mode, a section with less than 50% of the median primary section density (onsets/s) takes all of its notes from the fill stem, and the primary notes in that section are dropped.
  - Bar and section membership come from `sec_to_tick` and `tm["bar_ticks"]`.
  - The inputs are not mutated, and the output is sorted by time.
- RED: the test module failed to import (no stages/fill.py). GREEN: 11 tests pass. The full Python autochart suite (venv A) is 71/71.
- In `chart`, `--fill-stem` loads `onsets_<fill>.json` (audible and loud) and `fill_silent` runs before `quantize.build_expert`. chart_stats.json records `fill_stem`, `fill_mode` and `fill_share`. Drum fill notes keep `pitch: None` and a centroid, so lanes.py's centroid rule assigns their lanes. `--fill-stem` equal to `--stem` is rejected.
- Regression check: re-running the plan-05 Sea Shanty 2 command (`--delay-ms 72 --song-length-ms 127850`, output to build/) prints the same `CHART_OK … cdd1c284…0721`, and song.ini is byte-identical.
