# Phase 2.1 Plan 03: Charter decisions (contour lanes, reductions, structure) Summary

This plan adds deterministic stdlib functions for the charter's decisions: contour lanes (with the RESEARCH octave compromise and the VISIBLE SLIDE window), accent chords, 1/16-shortened sustains, Clone Hero Hard/Medium/Easy reductions that are subsets of the Expert ticks, and the section, solo and preview picks. 25 unit tests cover them on synthetic inputs.

## What was built

- **lanes.py**: the contour rule (step 0 / +-1 / +-2 for intervals of 5 or more semitones), the octave compromise, the slide rule (rising scale from lane 0 gives 0,1,2,3,3,3,1,2; the falling mirror gives 3,2,1,0,0,0,2,1), centroid pitch fallback, accent chords on Expert/Hard, sustains, and two metrics: direction_match and lane_step_hist. The module docstring explains the octave interpretation and the slide rule so plan 05 can copy it into the README.
- **reduce.py**: Hard (7/s cap, at most 2-note chords, the weaker half of chords become single notes), Medium (1/8 grid, share capped at 0.75, 5/s cap, at most 3% chords, all on downbeats), Easy (1/8 grid, greedy selection with at least a 1-beat gap, share capped at 0.55, 4/s cap, 3-lane contour or easy_lane_map, no chords, sustains kept), plus keep_share. Sparse grids keep every candidate and are never padded.
- **structure.py**: generic section names (Intro/Verse n/Chorus n/Section N/Outro), the solo window (densest window that is mostly instrumental, falling back to the densest window, or None), and the preview start (loudest section).

On the 64-bar synthetic test song: Hard keeps 97.7% (28 of 55 chords remain), Medium 73.4%, Easy 46.9%. Every lane-step histogram has a +-3 share of 0.
