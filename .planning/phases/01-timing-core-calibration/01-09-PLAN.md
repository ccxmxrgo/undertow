## Phase Goal

**As a** player who switches between speakers and headphones, **I want** the calibration wizard to tell me honestly what it knows about my output, detect hardware changes it can detect, and remind me to recalibrate after switching, **so that** my calibration offset matches the hardware I am playing on.

Purpose: CAL-01 device awareness is dead because `AudioServer.output_device` returns the alias "Default" on the Windows default device. Decision (orchestrator): no OS-level lookup in the shipped game; use Godot APIs and be honest about the limits.
Output: AudioDeviceInfo with a list/mix-rate/latency signature, Settings and wizard wiring, a recalibration reminder note, a narrowed Bluetooth marker list, clearer re-run UX, constrained button widths, tests.
</objective>
